# WWN Classes and Progression Tables

Complete class reference for character creation. All data from WWN 1 pp.18-21, 64; WWN 2 pp.66-87; WWN 5 pp.167-175; WWN 4 pp.344-355.

## Base Classes

### Warrior

Masters of combat, distinguished by their ability to deal and survive damage.

**Hit Die:** 1d6+2 per level

**Abilities:**
- **Killing Blow** — Add half your level (rounded up) to all damage rolls and Shock damage
- **Veteran's Luck** — Once per scene, force a reroll of a hit against you or turn a miss you made into a hit

**Focus Picks at Level 1:** 1 Any + 1 Warrior

**Progression:**

| Level | Hit Dice | Attack Bonus | New Focus Pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1d6+2 | +1 | 1 Any + 1 Warrior |
| 2 | 2d6+4 | +2 | +1 Any |
| 3 | 3d6+6 | +3 | — |
| 4 | 4d6+8 | +4 | — |
| 5 | 5d6+10 | +5 | +1 Any |
| 6 | 6d6+12 | +6 | — |
| 7 | 7d6+14 | +7 | +1 Any |
| 8 | 8d6+16 | +8 | — |
| 9 | 9d6+18 | +9 | — |
| 10 | 10d6+20 | +10 | +1 Any |

**XP Progression:** 0 / 1500 / 3000 / 6000 / 12000 / 24000 / 48000 / 96000 / 192000 / 384000

### Expert

Skilled professionals who excel outside of combat.

**Hit Die:** 1d6 per level

**Abilities:**
- **Masterful Expertise** — Once per scene, reroll a failed non-combat skill check
- **Quick Learner** — Gain 1 extra non-combat skill point per level

**Focus Picks at Level 1:** 1 Any + 1 Non-Combat

### Adventurer (Multi-Class)

A versatile hero who combines two partial classes.

**How it works:** Pick two partial classes. Each gives partial access to the full class's abilities. Hit die, attack bonus, and focus picks depend on the specific combination.

## Optional Partial Classes (WWN5)

These replace one of the standard partial classes (expert, warrior, or mage) in an Adventurer build.

### Accursed (replaces Partial Mage)

Eldritch pact-wielder with otherworldly blade and bolt powers.

- **Base Type:** Mage
- **Bonus Skill:** Magic
- **Combat Skill:** Magic (used for Blade/Bolt attack and damage rolls)
- **Effort Formula:** Magic skill + max(Int modifier, Cha modifier)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 2, Level 2: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 5: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 10: +1

**Adventurer combos with Accursed:**

| Combo | Hit Die | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Accursed + Expert | 1d6 | Masterful Expertise |
| Accursed + Warrior | 1d6+2 | Veteran's Luck (limited) |
| Accursed + Mage | 1d6-1 | (none) |

### Bard (replaces Partial Expert)

Performer charged with the Legacy's power through music, song, and speech.

- **Base Type:** Expert
- **Bonus Skill:** Perform
- **Effort Formula:** Perform skill + Cha modifier
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Special:** No Quick Learner. No bonus focus pick
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 2, Level 2: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 10: +1

**Adventurer combos with Bard:**

| Combo | Hit Die | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Bard + Warrior | 1d6+2 | Veteran's Luck (limited) |
| Bard + Mage | 1d6-1 | (none) |
| Bard + Expert | Not valid (both expert-type) | — |

### Mageslayer (replaces Partial Warrior)

Anti-magic combat specialist focused on countering spellcasters.

- **Base Type:** Warrior
- **Bonus Skill:** Magic
- **Effort Formula:** Magic skill + max(Int modifier, Con modifier)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Special:** No bonus combat focus. No warrior HP bonus. Fixed art progression (arts are assigned at each level, not chosen)
- **RESTRICTION:** Cannot pair with any spellcasting class (mage, accursed, invoker, skinshifter, duelist, beastmaster, blood_priest, thought_noble)

**Fixed Art Progression:**

| Level | Arts Gained |
|---|---|
| 1 | Antimage, Magebane |
| 2 | Witchfinder, Spellshield |
| 3 | Disrupt Sorcery |
| 4 | Know Your Prey |
| 5 | Share the Pain |
| 6 | Dispel Enchantment |
| 7 | Ward Ally |
| 8 | Immaculate Body |
| 9 | Immaculate Mind |
| 10 | Absolute Negation |

**Adventurer combos with Mageslayer:**

| Combo | Hit Die | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Mageslayer + Expert | 1d6 | Masterful Expertise |
| Mageslayer + Warrior | 1d6+2 | Veteran's Luck (limited) |

### Wise (replaces Partial Expert)

Low-magic scholar, priest, or oracle for no-magic or low-magic settings.

- **Base Type:** Expert
- **Bonus Skill:** Pray (default; can be pray/know/magic/survive per GM)
- **Effort Formula:** None (Wise do NOT use Effort)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Special:** No Quick Learner. No bonus focus pick
- **Art Categories:** General, Divination, Curses and Blessings
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 1, Level 2: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 5: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 10: +1

**Adventurer combos with Wise:**

| Combo | Hit Die | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Wise + Warrior | 1d6+2 | Veteran's Luck (limited) |
| Wise + Mage | 1d6-1 | (none) |
| Wise + Expert | Not valid (both expert-type) | — |

## Gyre Partial Classes (WWN4)

These are setting-specific classes that replace Partial Mage. All use the standard Partial Mage combo tables (1d6 with Expert, 1d6+1 with Warrior) unless noted.

### Invoker (replaces Partial Mage)

Adunic spell-point caster using High Magic spells without Vancian slots.

- **Base Type:** Mage
- **Bonus Skill:** Magic
- **Effort Formula:** None (uses spell points, not Effort)
- **Has Spells:** Yes (spell-point system instead of prepared/cast slots)
- **Can be Full Class:** Yes

### Skinshifter (replaces Partial Mage)

Darian shapeshifter who masters alternate animal and humanoid forms.

- **Base Type:** Mage
- **Bonus Skill:** Survive
- **Effort Formula:** Survive skill + max(Con modifier, Cha modifier)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Form Bonus by Level:** 1:+1, 2:+1, 3:+2, 4:+2, 5:+3, 6:+3, 7:+4, 8:+4, 9:+5, 10:+6
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 2, Level 2: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 5: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 9: +1
- **Mandatory Starting Art:** Change Form

### Duelist (replaces Partial Mage)

Kistian martial artist specializing in single-weapon fencing and evasion.

- **Base Type:** Mage
- **Bonus Skill:** Stab
- **Effort Formula:** Stab skill + max(Dex modifier, Int modifier)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Restriction:** Cannot benefit from arts while wearing medium/heavy armor or large shield
- **Fragility Flaw:** When paired with Warrior, hit die is 1d6 (not 1d6+1)
- **Favored Weapon Bonus by Level:** 1:+1, 2:+1, 3:+2, 4:+2, 5:+3, 6:+3, 7:+4, 8:+4, 9:+5, 10:+5
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 2, Level 2: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 5: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 9: +1

### Beastmaster (replaces Partial Mage)

Llaigisan animal companion specialist who bonds with a loyal beast.

- **Base Type:** Mage
- **Bonus Skill:** Survive
- **Effort Formula:** Survive skill + max(Wis modifier, Cha modifier)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 2, Level 2: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 5: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 10: +1
- **Required Starting Art:** Bind Companion

### Blood Priest (replaces Partial Mage)

Sarulite divine miracle worker who channels faith through vocalized prayer.

- **Base Type:** Mage
- **Bonus Skill:** Pray
- **Effort Formula:** Pray skill + max(Wis modifier, Cha modifier)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Restriction:** Miracles require vocalization
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 2, Level 2: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 5: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 10: +1

### Thought Noble (replaces Partial Mage)

Vothite psychic who manipulates minds with invisible mental arts.

- **Base Type:** Mage
- **Bonus Skill:** Notice
- **Effort Formula:** Notice skill + max(Int modifier, Wis modifier)
- **Has Spells:** No
- **Restriction:** All arts invisible to non-magical senses. Successful Mental save = target immune for rest of scene
- **Art Progression:** Level 1: 2, Level 2: +1, Level 3: +1, Level 4: +1, Level 5: +1, Level 6: +1, Level 7: +1, Level 8: +1, Level 9: +1, Level 10: +1

## Standard Gyre Combo Tables

All Gyre classes (except Duelist) use these standard tables when paired:

**Gyre + Expert:** Hit Die 1d6, Abilities: Masterful Expertise. Same progression as Partial Expert / Partial Mage table.

**Gyre + Warrior:** Hit Die 1d6+1, Abilities: Veteran's Luck (limited). Same progression as Partial Mage / Partial Warrior table.

**Duelist + Warrior (exception):** Hit Die 1d6 (fragility flaw, not 1d6+1). Uses Partial Mage / Partial Warrior attack bonus progression but with 1d6 hit die.
