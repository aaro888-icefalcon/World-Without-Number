# WWN Magic Traditions and Class Arts

Complete reference for all traditions and partial class arts. Data from WWN 1 pp.60-93, WWN 2 pp.1-55, WWN 4 pp.344-355, WWN 5 pp.167-175.

## Magic Traditions (for Mage and Partial Mage)

### High Mage

Classical wizard tradition. Masters of versatile arcane spells learned through study and practice.

**Restriction:** Cannot cast spells while wearing armor heavier than normal clothing.
**Has Spells:** Yes
**Available to:** Full Mage or Partial Mage

**Arts:**

| Art | Effort | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Arcane Lexicon | None | Can read any language and identify magical writing. Gain Magic-0 if unskilled. |
| Counter Magic | Scene | As an Instant action, commit Effort for the scene to negate a spell targeting you or an ally within sight. |
| Empowered Sorcery | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene to maximize the dice of a spell's damage or healing. |
| Hang Sorcery | Day | Commit Effort for the day to prepare a spell for instant casting later. Casting the hung spell is an Instant action. |
| Inexorable Effect | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. A target that saves against your spell still suffers a lesser version of the effect. |
| Preparatory Countermagic | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene as an Instant when you detect an incoming spell. You and allies within 20 feet gain +4 on saves against it. |
| Reflexive Cast | Day | Commit Effort for the day to cast a spell as an Instant action instead of a Main Action. The spell must be level 1. |
| Restrained Casting | None | You can shape your area-effect spells to exclude up to 1 ally per caster level from the effect. |
| Sense Magic | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene to detect magical effects, auras, and enchantments within 100 feet. |
| Suppress Magic | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene to temporarily suppress a magical effect you can see. |
| Swift Casting | Day | Commit Effort for the day to cast a prepared spell without losing it from your prepared list. |
| Ward Allies | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene to grant an ally within 50 feet a +2 bonus to saves against magic. |

**Spells:**

*Level 1:*

|---|---|

| Hartley's Hasty Habiliment | Instantly don armor and equipment stored in a prepared extradimensional pocket. |
| Imperceptible Cerebral Divination | Read surface thoughts of a target within 100 feet for one scene. Mental save negates. |
| Lexicon of the Unspoken Name | Understand and speak any language for one scene. |
| Mark of the Last Heir | Place a magical mark on an object or location. You can sense its direction and distance for 1 day. |
| The Omniscient Mote | Create a mote of light you can see and hear through. It moves at your will within 100 feet. Lasts one scene. |
| Perfect Transparency | Turn invisible for one scene. Attacking or casting another spell ends the invisibility. |

### Elementalist

Wielder of primal elemental forces — fire, water, earth, air, and combinations thereof.

**Restriction:** Cannot cast spells while wearing metal armor.
**Has Spells:** Yes
**Available to:** Full Mage or Partial Mage

**Arts:**

| Art | Effort | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Elemental Resilience | None | Gain resistance to one chosen element. Take half damage from that element. Gain Magic-0 if unskilled. |
| Elemental Blast | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Hurl a bolt of elemental energy: 1d8 damage per 2 caster levels, 200 ft range. |
| Elemental Sparks | None | Produce minor elemental effects at will: light a candle, chill a drink, stir a breeze, move a handful of earth. |
| Pavis of Elements | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Create an elemental shield granting +2 AC for the scene. |
| Elemental Weapon | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Imbue a weapon with elemental energy: +1d6 elemental damage per hit. |
| Beckoned Deluge | Day | Commit Effort for the day. Create a 20-foot-radius elemental area effect: 1d6 damage per caster level. |
| Elemental Aegis | Day | Commit Effort for the day. Grant an ally immunity to one element for the scene. |

| Hands of the Earth | Stone hands erupt from the ground and grasp a target. Physical save or immobilized for 1 round. |

| Stone Sense | Sense vibrations through stone/earth within 100 feet. Detect hidden creatures, tunnels, and structural weaknesses. |

### Necromancer

Master of death magic, undead creation, and the manipulation of life force.

**Restrictions:** Social penalty: -1 reaction rolls in civilized areas where necromancy is known. Cannot cast spells while wearing metal armor.
**Has Spells:** Yes
**Available to:** Full Mage or Partial Mage

**Arts:**

| Art | Effort | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Charnel Breath | None | Immune to diseases and poisons. Can detect undead within 100 feet. Gain Magic-0 if unskilled. |
| Command the Dead | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Command unintelligent undead within 100 feet. HD of controlled undead cannot exceed caster level x 2. |
| Consume Life Energy | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Touch a freshly dead creature to heal 1d6+level HP. |
| Red Harvest | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. When you kill a creature, gain temporary HP equal to its HD. |
| Raise the Dead | Day | Commit Effort for the day. Raise a corpse as an undead servant with HD equal to half your level. |
| Pale Countenance | None | Undead do not attack you unless commanded to. You can communicate with unintelligent undead. |
| Life Bridge | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Transfer HP from yourself to a touched ally, or drain HP from a touched enemy. |

**Spells:**

*Level 1:*

| Spell | Description |
|---|---|
| Beckoning of the Hungry Grave | Raise 1 HD of undead per caster level from available corpses. They serve for one scene. |
| Blade of Black Entropy | Imbue a weapon with necrotic energy. +1d6 necrotic damage per hit for one scene. Heals wielder for half. |
| Caul of Night | Create an area of magical darkness in a 30-foot radius. Only you can see through it. Lasts one scene. |
| Choking Doom of Dust | Dust fills a target's lungs. 1d8 damage per round until Physical save succeeds. Max 3 rounds. |
| Cold Grasp of the Departed | Spectral hand chills a target for 2d6 cold/necrotic damage. Physical save for half. |
| Enfeebling Wave | Wave of enervation in a 20-foot cone. -2 to attack rolls and damage for 1 round. Physical save negates. |
| Speak with Dead | Question a corpse. It answers up to 3 questions truthfully from its knowledge in life. |
| The Ravening Swarm | Summon a swarm of skeletal vermin. 1d6 damage per round to all in a 10-foot area. Lasts one scene. |
| Terrifying Visage | Your face becomes terrifyingly gaunt. All enemies within 30 feet must Mental save or flee for 1 round. |
| Withering Curse | Curse a target within 100 feet. -1 to all rolls for one scene. Mental save negates. |

*Level 2:*

| Spell | Description |
|---|---|
| Animate Revenant | Create a powerful undead servant from a corpse. HD equal to your level. Serves for 1 day. |
| Deathly Convocation | Commune with the spirits of the dead in the area. Learn one fact about local history per question (3 max). |
| Soul Shackles | Bind a dying creature's soul. They cannot die while bound but suffer 1d6 damage per round. Mental save breaks. |

*Level 3:*

| Spell | Description |
|---|---|
| Plague Wind | Poisonous wind in a 40-foot cone. 4d6 necrotic damage and poisoned for 1 day. Physical save for half and no poison. |
| Reign of the Unliving | Raise all corpses within 100 feet as undead servants. Total HD cannot exceed your level x 3. Lasts one scene. |

*Level 4:*

| Spell | Description |
|---|---|
| Legion of the Restless Dead | Raise up to 20 HD of undead from available corpses. They serve until destroyed or dismissed. Maximum 3 days duration. |
| Soul Harvest | When a creature dies within 100 feet, capture its soul energy. Heal 2d8 HP and recover one committed Effort. Lasts one scene. |

*Level 5:*

| Spell | Description |
|---|---|
| Dominion of the Grave | All undead within 200 feet fall under your absolute control. Living creatures in the area must Mental save or be paralyzed with dread for 1 round. Control lasts one scene. |

### Healer (Partial Only)

Magically gifted physician who channels supernatural healing power. Partial tradition only.

**Restriction:** Must not deliberately cause lethal harm to a creature that has not attacked them or their allies.
**Has Spells:** No
**Available to:** Adventurer (Partial) only

**Arts:**

| Art | Effort | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Healing Touch | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. As a Main Action, heal a touched ally for 2d6+level HP. Gain Heal-0 if unskilled. |
| Purge Ailment | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Cure one disease, poison, or mundane physical ailment in a touched creature. |
| Vital Sense | None | Can diagnose any disease, poison, or physical ailment by touch. Sense life and death within 50 feet. |
| Empowered Healer | Day | Commit Effort for the day. Your next Healing Touch heals maximum dice and removes one condition. |
| Stabilize | None | As a Main Action, automatically stabilize a dying creature within touch range. No check required. |
| Shield of Life | Day | Commit Effort for the day. An ally within sight cannot be reduced below 1 HP for one round. |

### Vowed (Partial Only)

Ascetic martial artist who channels supernatural power through bodily discipline. Partial tradition only.

**Restrictions:** Cannot wear armor. Cannot use weapons other than unarmed attacks and monk weapons (staff, dagger, short sword).
**Has Spells:** No
**Available to:** Adventurer (Partial) only

**Arts:**

| Art | Effort | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Unarmed Might | None | Unarmed attacks deal 1d8 damage and are not considered Less Lethal. Gain Punch-0 if unskilled. |
| Unarmored Defense | None | While unarmored, AC equals 10 + half level (rounded up) + Wis modifier. |
| Flurry of Blows | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Make two unarmed attacks as a single Main Action. |
| Iron Skin | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Gain damage reduction 2 against all physical attacks. |
| Inner Force | Day | Commit Effort for the day. Your next unarmed attack deals maximum damage. |
| Wind Step | Scene | Commit Effort for the scene. Move up to 30 extra feet as a free action once per round. |
