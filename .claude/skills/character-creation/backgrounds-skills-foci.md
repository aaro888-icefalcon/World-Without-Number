**Starting skills from background:**
1. Background free skill (automatically at level 0)
2. Pick 2 from the background's quick skills list (each at level 0)
3. 1 free pick: any skill at level 0

**Skill stacking:** If a skill is gained twice at level 0, it becomes level 1. Maximum skill level at character creation is 1.

## All Skills

Skill check: 2d6 + skill level + attribute modifier >= difficulty

**Difficulties:** 6 (simple), 8 (significant challenge), 10 (gifted expert), 12 (master-level), 14 (near-superhuman)

**Skill levels:** -1 (unskilled), 0 (competent), 1 (veteran), 2 (city-best), 3 (kingdom-best), 4 (world-best)

## Foci

Characters get 2 focus picks at level 1. Focus type determines class eligibility.

### Type Restrictions

| Type | Who Can Take It |
|---|---|
| any | All classes |
| warrior | Warriors or Partial Warriors only |
| mage_only | Mages or Partial Mages only |
| non_mage | Cannot be taken by Mages or Partial Mages |
| expert_only | Experts or Partial Experts only |

### Core Foci (WWN 1 pp.22-31)

#### Alert
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Notice as a bonus skill. You cannot be surprised, nor can others use the Execution Attack option on you. +1 bonus to your side's initiative roll (doesn't stack with other Alert PCs). If rolling individually, roll twice and take the better result.
- **Level 2:** You always act first in a combat round unless someone else involved is also this Alert.

#### Armored Magic
**Type:** mage_only
- **Level 1:** You can cast spells or use arts while wearing armor with an Encumbrance value of no more than two. You can use a shield while casting, provided your other hand is empty for gesturing.
- **Level 2:** You can cast spells while wearing armor of any Encumbrance. You can cast spells while both your hands are full, though not bound.

#### Armsmaster
**Type:** warrior
- **Level 1:** Gain Stab as a bonus skill. You can Ready a Stowed melee or thrown weapon as an Instant action. You may add your Stab skill level to a melee or thrown weapon's damage roll or Shock damage, assuming it has any to begin with.
- **Level 2:** The Shock from your melee attacks always treats the target as if they have AC 10. Gain a +1 bonus to hit with all thrown or melee attacks.

#### Artisan
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Craft as a bonus skill. Your Craft skill is treated as one level higher (max 5) for crafting and maintaining mods. Mods you build require one fewer unit of arcane salvage (min 1). Your Craft skill applies to any normal crafting profession's work.
- **Level 2:** The first mod you add to an item requires no Maintenance and only half the silver piece cost. You automatically succeed at building masterwork gear. Once per month reduce a mod's salvage cost by one further unit (min 0).

#### Assassin
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Sneak as a bonus skill. You can conceal an object no larger than a knife from anything less invasive than a strip search. You can draw it as an On Turn action, and your point-blank thrown or melee attacks during a surprise round with it cannot miss. Execution Attack advantages per p.44.
- **Level 2:** You can take a Move action on the same round as you make an Execution Attack, splitting it before and after the attack. This movement is too quick to alert a victim or be hindered by bodyguards.

#### Authority
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Lead as a bonus skill. Once per day, make a request from a non-hostile NPC with a Cha/Lead check against their Morale score. On success, they comply if the request is not significantly harmful or extremely uncharacteristic.
- **Level 2:** NPCs directly led by you gain a Morale and hit roll bonus equal to your Lead skill and +1 on all skill checks. Followers and henchmen will not act against your interests unless under extreme pressure.

#### Close Combatant
**Type:** warrior
- **Level 1:** Gain any combat skill as a bonus skill. You can use knife-sized thrown weapons in melee without penalty. You ignore Shock damage from melee assailants even if unarmored, but this disrupts any spellcasting you do that round.
- **Level 2:** The Shock damage from your melee attacks treats all targets as if they were AC 10. The Fighting Withdrawal combat action is treated as an On Turn action for you.

#### Connected
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Connect as a bonus skill. If you've spent at least a week in a not-entirely-hostile location, you'll have built a web of contacts who can provide you with useful information and minor services. Once per session, you can find a contact who can provide a useful piece of information or a minor favor.
- **Level 2:** Your contacts are significantly more capable and loyal. Contacts won't betray you unless under severe duress. Once per session, a contact can provide a significant favor or piece of rare information.

#### Cultured Traveler
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Connect as a bonus skill. You can speak, read, and write three additional languages. +1 bonus to reaction rolls in foreign cultures.
- **Level 2:** You can attempt to communicate basic concepts in any language after a day of exposure. +2 bonus to reaction rolls in foreign cultures (replaces +1).

#### Deadeye
**Type:** warrior
- **Level 1:** Gain Shoot as a bonus skill. You can Ready a Stowed ranged weapon as an Instant action. You suffer no penalties for shooting at targets at the weapon's maximum range. +1 to hit and +1 to damage with ranged weapons.
- **Level 2:** You can reload a ranged weapon as an On Turn action. Ranged attacks ignore cover short of full cover. +2 to hit with ranged weapons (replaces +1).

#### Developed Attribute
**Type:** any | **Repeatable:** Yes (different attributes)
- **Level 1:** +2 to one attribute score (max 18).

#### Die Hard
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Survive as a bonus skill. When reduced to 0 HP, you remain conscious and can act, though you're Frail. You automatically stabilize at the end of your round.
- **Level 2:** Once per scene when reduced to 0 HP or less, you instead remain at 1 HP. You can still be killed by additional damage after this.

#### Diplomat
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Convince as a bonus skill. +1 bonus to reaction rolls. Once per scene, you can attempt to defuse a hostile NPC situation with a Cha/Convince check.
- **Level 2:** +2 bonus to reaction rolls (replaces +1). Your defusing attempts can work even against enemies currently in combat.

#### Gifted Chiurgeon
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Heal as a bonus skill. You can stabilize a dying creature as an On Turn action. Once per day per patient, you can heal 1d6+Heal skill HP with ten minutes of treatment.
- **Level 2:** Once per scene, your medical treatment can cure a poison or disease. Your daily healing increases to 2d6+Heal skill.

#### Henchkeeper
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Maximum henchmen count +4. Henchmen gain +2 Morale. Once per session, you can find a local hireling willing to work for you.
- **Level 2:** Henchmen gain +4 Morale (replaces +2). Once per session, you can attract a specialist hireling with a useful skill.

#### Impervious Defense
**Type:** warrior
- **Level 1:** Gain any combat skill as a bonus skill. While wearing armor, +1 AC. You are immune to Shock damage from enemies with fewer hit dice than your level.
- **Level 2:** +2 AC while wearing armor (replaces +1). You are immune to Shock from all enemies.

#### Nullifier
**Type:** non_mage
- **Level 1:** Gain Magic as a bonus skill. +2 bonus to all saving throws against magical effects. Once per scene, you can reroll a failed saving throw against a magical effect.
- **Level 2:** +4 bonus to saves against magic (replaces +2). Once per day, you can completely negate a magical effect targeting you.

#### Poisoner
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Heal or Survive as a bonus skill. You can create 3 doses of poison per day from common materials. Applying poison is an On Turn action. Your poisons have +2 to their save DC.
- **Level 2:** Your poisons can mimic specific symptoms. You can create antidotes for any poison you've examined. Poison save DC +4 (replaces +2).

#### Rider
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Ride as a bonus skill. +2 to mounted skill checks. Your mount gains +2 AC. Mounted charge: +2 damage.
- **Level 2:** +4 to mounted skill checks (replaces +2). Your mount gains +4 AC (replaces +2). You can fight from a mount with no penalties.

#### Shocking Assault
**Type:** warrior
- **Level 1:** +2 Shock damage with all melee and thrown weapons. On a missed melee attack, you still inflict Shock if the target qualifies.
- **Level 2:** Your Shock damage ignores shields. +4 Shock damage (replaces +2).

#### Sniper's Eye
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Shoot as a bonus skill. You suffer no penalty for shooting into melee. You can make ranged Execution Attacks.
- **Level 2:** Once per scene, treat a ranged attack as if the target had AC 10. Your ranged attacks ignore non-total cover.

#### Special Origin
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain a custom ability approved by the GM. May confer minor non-human traits.

#### Specialist
**Type:** any | **Repeatable:** Yes (different skills)
- **Level 1:** Gain any non-combat skill as a bonus skill. +1 on checks with that specific skill.
- **Level 2:** +2 on checks with the chosen skill (replaces +1). You can reroll one failed check with this skill per session.

#### Spirit Familiar
**Type:** mage_only
- **Level 1:** Gain Magic as a bonus skill. You have a minor spirit servant that can perform simple tasks. It grants +1 to one skill of your choice. It can deliver touch-range spells at up to 100 feet.
- **Level 2:** Your familiar can scout independently and communicate telepathically. Its skill bonus increases to +2.

#### Stoic
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain any skill as a bonus skill. +2 on saving throws against mind-affecting effects. You are immune to fear.
- **Level 2:** +4 on saves against mind-affecting (replaces +2). You can spend a Main Action to remove a mind-affecting condition from yourself.

#### Trapmaster
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Notice as a bonus skill. +2 to find and disarm traps. Once per scene, you automatically detect a trap before triggering it.
- **Level 2:** You can set traps that deal 2d6 damage. +4 to find/disarm (replaces +2). Your traps are harder to detect (+2 DC).

#### Unarmed Combatant
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Punch as a bonus skill. Your unarmed attacks deal 1d6+Punch damage and are not Less Lethal. +1 AC while fighting unarmed.
- **Level 2:** Unarmed damage increases to 1d8+Punch. +2 AC unarmed (replaces +1). You can Grapple as an On Turn action.

#### Unique Gift
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** A custom supernatural ability approved by the GM.

#### Valiant Defender
**Type:** warrior
- **Level 1:** Once per round, you can use Screen Ally as an Instant action instead of a Move action. +2 AC when Screening.
- **Level 2:** You can Screen any number of allies within reach. Attacks that would hit a Screened ally hit you at -2 to their damage.

#### Wanderer
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Survive as a bonus skill. +1 overland travel speed. You always find sufficient food and water in wilderness areas.
- **Level 2:** You cannot become lost in wilderness. You can track creatures by sign. +2 overland speed (replaces +1).

#### Whirlwind Assault
**Type:** warrior
- **Level 1:** Once per scene, you can attack every adjacent enemy with a single Main Action. Each attack is rolled separately.
- **Level 2:** You can use Whirlwind Assault once per round instead of once per scene.

#### Wilderness Tracker
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** Gain Survive as a bonus skill. You can track creatures by sign and trail. +2 to tracking checks.
- **Level 2:** You can track across any terrain, including water and stone. +4 to tracking (replaces +2). You can determine the number and type of creatures in a tracked group.

#### Xenoblooded
**Type:** any
- **Level 1:** A custom minor alien or fey ability. Gain one unusual physical trait.
