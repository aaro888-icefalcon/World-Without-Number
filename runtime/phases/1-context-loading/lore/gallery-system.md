# The Gallery System

Cross-cutting lore reference for the nine-level dungeon complex beneath NYC in the Carven Peaks.

## Overview

The Gallery System is a nine-level subterranean complex descending through multiple civilizational layers beneath New York City. The upper levels (1-3) were carved by the Pale Empire within the last three millennia. Beneath them lie far older constructions of the Grand Harmony Outsiders (Levels 4-6), predating the Pale Empire by centuries or more. Deeper still, the Throne-Barrow (Levels 7-8) houses the Pale Emperor's personal megastructure, and Level 9 contains the Cage — the prison of a hostile Imperator.

The Pale Emperor's undead carvers are present throughout Levels 1-6, their behavior shifting from passive to confrontational as one descends toward the containment infrastructure they maintain.

Every level connects laterally to other Deeps, making the Gallery System a nexus for underground travel across the boroughs and beyond.

---

## Levels 1-3: Pale Empire Construction

Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 form the upper gallery — the most accessible and most explored portion of the system.

**Age:** 0-3,000 years.

**Architecture:** Carved stone with intricate patterns, engineered water channels, workshops, and temples. Recognizably humanoid in scale and purpose.

### Inhabitants — Feral Undead

The upper levels are infested with feral undead: degraded remnants of the Pale Empire's population. These are mindless predatory revenants — desiccated corpses that move with jerky, insectile speed. They attack living beings on sight, ignore the Emperor's carvers entirely, and avoid concentrated light.

Density increases with depth:

| Level | Density per Section | Behavior |
|-------|-------------------|----------|
| L1 | 1-3 (sparse) | Isolated encounters, often avoidable |
| L2 | 3-6 (moderate) | Small groups, territorial |
| L3 | 6-12 (dense) | Organized packs, coordinated pursuit |

### Carver Behavior (Levels 1-3)

Passive. The Emperor's carvers ignore humans completely and reroute around obstructions without acknowledgment. They continue their millennia-old work as though living intruders do not exist.

### Loot

- Silver-colored alloy metalwork — harder and lighter than steel, but cannot be smelted by NYC's current technology.
- Carved stone objects of unknown ritual or practical purpose.
- Crystal vessels, some retaining residual magical properties: purification, preservation, illumination.
- Occasional functional Workings (rare, especially at L1).

### Hazards

Structural instability, flooded sections, gas pockets, and degraded Working-traps that may still trigger unpredictably.

### Fatality Rates

| Level | Rate |
|-------|------|
| L1 | 2-4% |
| L2 | 4-7% |
| L3 | 7-12% |

## Levels 4-6: Grand Harmony Outsider Construction

**Age:** 1,500-3,500+ years. Predates the Pale Empire.

### Level 4 — Civilian

**Architecture:** Grown crystal — translucent, warm to touch, pulsing with faint internal light. This is not carved stone; the structures appear to have been cultivated or extruded by processes outside human understanding.

**Spatial anomalies:** The geometry of Level 4 challenges human perception. Corridors curve imperceptibly so that straight-line navigation fails. Chambers feel larger inside than their external dimensions suggest. Ceilings shift position when not directly observed.

**Inhabitants:**

- *Burrower colonies* — blind, echolocating creatures roughly human-sized. Some colonies are trade-willing and can be negotiated with; others are hostile. Distinguishing between the two requires experience or careful observation.
- *Outsider dormant automata* — inactive machines of unknown function scattered throughout. Their activation triggers are unknown. [UNKNOWN]

**The Floor-Map:** A massive chamber containing a three-dimensional crystalline model of the entire Gallery System. Current exploration has mapped less than 5% of what the model depicts. The floor-map is one of the most strategically valuable discoveries in the Gallery System.

**Fatality rate:** 12-18%.

### Level 5 — Military/Sorcerous

**Architecture:** Darker crystal interlaced with metallic structures. The spaces are clearly functional — barracks, arsenals, laboratories — but designed for Outsider physiology and cognition.

**Spatial anomalies:** Non-Euclidean geometry. Rooms connect in ways that violate three-dimensional spatial logic. Mapping is extremely difficult; experienced delvers rely on markers and rote memorization rather than cartography.

**Inhabitants:**

- *Active automata patrols* — unlike the dormant units of Level 4, these are operational. Their patrol routes are learnable and their detection patterns evadable with careful observation and timing.

**Carver behavior (Level 5):** Obstructive. Carvers actively block passages, physically interposing themselves to prevent deeper descent. They do not attack but will not yield.

**Fatality rate:** 20-30%.

### Level 6 — Containment Transition

**Architecture:** Hybrid construction. The oldest Outsider crystal is overlaid by Pale Empire containment infrastructure — massive inscribed stone blocks sealing downward passages. This is the interface layer where two civilizations' engineering intersects.

**Purpose:** Level 6 houses the first containment seals and the functional magical engineering that maintains the Imperator's cage below. The containment system is still active and represents a critical piece of infrastructure for the safety of everything above.

**Carver behavior (Level 6):** Confrontational. Carvers form living walls to block access and will use their tools as weapons against intruders who press forward. This is the only level range where carvers pose a direct physical threat.

**Fatality rate:** 30-40%.

### Loot (Levels 4-6)

| Level | Loot Profile |
|-------|-------------|
| L4 | Crystal devices, organic technology, navigational instruments. Trade value is civilization-altering — any of these artifacts would reshape NYC's technological capabilities. |
| L5 | Military-grade artifacts: energy-channeling weapons, kinetic-absorbing armor. Extraordinarily dangerous to recover and extraordinarily valuable. |
| L6 | Containment artifacts, deepest Outsider technology. Removing these items risks destabilizing the seal infrastructure. |

## Levels 7-8: The Throne-Barrow

Level 7 and Level 8 compose the Pale Emperor's personal megastructure.

**Architecture:** The Pale Emperor's personal megastructure. Stone patterns so fine they appear to move in peripheral vision. Corridors span 30 feet across; chambers are scaled for thousands. The sheer scale communicates that this space was built for a singular, monumental purpose.

**The Throne-Barrow as Machine:** The carved patterns covering every surface are not decorative. They are functional components of an enormous mechanism that maintains the Imperator's cage, monitors the entire Gallery System above, and preserves remnants of the Pale Emperor's consciousness. The Throne-Barrow is, in essence, a building-sized Working.

### The Fragmentary Consciousness

The Pale Emperor's mind has degraded over three millennia into partial echoes distributed throughout Levels 7-8. These manifest as:

- Whispered word-fragments at the edge of hearing.
- Emotional impressions — grief, determination, exhaustion, fear — that wash over attuned individuals.
- Fragmentary attempts at communication for characters with sufficient sensitivity or magical attunement.

Each echo remembers a different aspect of the Emperor's existence. No single encounter reveals the full picture. Assembling a complete understanding of the Emperor's purpose, history, and current state would require contact with multiple fragments across multiple characters' lifetimes.

**Fatality rate:** 40%+.

---

## Level 9: The Cage

The imprisoned hostile Imperator's domain. Reality is unstable here — physical laws behave inconsistently, distances shift, cause and effect become unreliable. The Imperator's will presses against everything within the Cage, a constant psychic weight that intensifies with proximity to its center.

Servitors are generated within the Cage and pushed upward through breaches in the containment seals. These entities are the ultimate source of many threats encountered in the levels above.

No character has reached Level 9 within any plausible early-campaign window. The Cage is a long-term campaign destination, not an accessible location. [UNKNOWN — the Imperator's nature, its original imprisonment, and the full extent of its influence remain deliberately undefined.]

## Deep Connections

Every level of the Gallery System connects laterally to other underground networks. Known connections:

| Access Point | Connection | Description |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| L2 (Queens) | Pre-Colligation cavern system | Flooded caverns with sealed, air-pocketed chambers. Navigation requires waterproofing and breath management. |
| L2 (Manhattan) | Deep Lord transit-tunnel | Dry tunnel of ~2500 BL construction, extending southeast. Purpose and terminus unknown. |
| L3 (Bronx) | Gebed Mur underground waterways | Possible covert route to Manthva. Higher density of organized undead from that direction. |
| L3 (Staten Island) | Verdancy root-infiltrated galleries | Possible route to Suldant cathedral-cities. Environment is a hybrid of carved stone and living plant matter. |
| L4 | Grand Harmony satellite installations | Multiple connections to other Outsider facilities. Scope and locations largely unexplored. |

## Borough-Specific Gallery Access

Access points to the Gallery System vary significantly by borough:

**Manhattan:** 40-60 cavern-wall mouths provide the densest and most direct upper-level access. Manhattan is the primary entry point for organized delving operations.

**Queens / Eastern Brooklyn:** Galleries run parallel to the eastern river system and include underground waterways. Access is wetter and more hazardous but less contested.

**Bronx:** Adjacent to the Gebed Mur underground waterways. Expeditions entering from the Bronx encounter a higher density of organized undead — these are not feral remnants but more structured threats originating from or influenced by Manthva.

**Staten Island / Western Brooklyn:** Portions of the Gallery System here have been partially colonized by Verdancy root systems, creating a hybrid carved-stone and living-plant environment. Navigation and hazard profiles differ substantially from the standard gallery experience.
