# The Imperator and Surge Dynamics

## The Caged Entity
A being of near-supreme magical power imprisoned below the throne-barrow (Gallery Levels 7-8). Conscious, hostile, actively working to escape. Perceives gallery activity through unknown senses. Aware of NYC's presence. Creates varied servitors from raw materials to breach seals from below.

## Servitor Types
- **Fast bipedal scouts:** Gallery-corridor speed, reconnaissance
- **Armored brutes:** Seal-battering capability
- **Infiltrators:** Micro-fracture exploitation, slip through tiny gaps
- **Adaptive variants:** Develop counter-measures against NYC's defenses over time — the Imperator learns

## Surge Dynamics

### Day 1 Surge
Transport event's Legacy disruption weakens seals momentarily. Servitors breach into cavern and outer-borough gallery entrances within hours. 400-1,000 dead. Contained within 24-48 hours by carver seal-repair.

### Minor Surges
1-6 servitors breach into Levels 5-6, usually contained by carvers. Frequency: every 1-2 weeks (increasing as seals degrade from gallery activity).

### Moderate Surges
15-40 servitors reach Levels 2-3, requiring military response. Frequency: every 6-10 weeks (increasing).

### Major Surges
50+ servitors reach Level 1 or the cavern. Existential events requiring full mobilization. Frequency: every 6-12 months (increasing).

## Escalation Mechanism
Gallery exploration degrades seals. Deeper exploration produces more degradation. Each Level 6+ seal breach measurably increases subsequent surge intensity. The system oscillates around an equilibrium depth-ceiling that advances slowly as NYC's defensive capability improves.

## Containment Seals
Located at Level 6 (Containment Transition zone). Massive inscribed stone constructions blocking downward passages. The inscribed patterns are functional magical engineering maintaining the cage.

Properties:
- Each breach requires significant physical effort
- Breach produces immediate consequences: carver convergence to repair (48-72 hours) and potential Imperator surge
- Self-repairing: each successive breach triggers stronger reconstruction
- Multiple redundant seals (exact number [UNKNOWN])

## The Pale Emperor's Role
The Pale Emperor (whose fragmentary consciousness persists in Levels 7-8) built the containment system ~3,000 years ago. The undead carvers are part of this system — they maintain the galleries and repair seal breaches. The Emperor's echoes distributed through the throne-barrow architecture remember fragments of why the cage was built, what the Imperator is, and what happens if it escapes.
