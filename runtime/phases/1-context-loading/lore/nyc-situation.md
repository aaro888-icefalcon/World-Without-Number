# NYC Dynamic Start Situation

Cross-cutting lore reference for NYC's dynamic start situation and timeline pressures. This document covers the material conditions at transport, the cascade of crises that unfold in the first weeks, and the structural pressures that shape the campaign's medium- and long-term arcs.

## Initial Material State (T=0)

- **Population**: 9.5 million (8.3M residents + 1.2M commuters/transients)
- **Functional**: physical structures, bridges, tunnels, hand tools, simple mechanical devices, printed books, clothing, bicycles, unpowered boats, glass/ceramics/metal goods, firearms (NYPD ~36,000 sidearms; civilian ~1-2M; National Guard armory), preserved food ~25,000 metric tons (~3 days normal consumption, 8-10 days rationed at 800 cal/day)
- **Permanently inoperative**: all electronics, motors, generators, engines, modern medical equipment, water pumps, refrigeration, communications, elevators, HVAC
- **Underground lake fish**: sustain 50,000-100,000 people only. Eastern valley agriculture is permanently insufficient for total population — NYC always requires Amundi imports to survive.

## Immediate Pressures (Days 1-14)

### The Surge (Day 1)

Imperator servitors breach galleries within hours of transport. Between 400 and 1,000 people die before carver seal-repair contains the incursion within 24-48 hours. This establishes the galleries as an active, persistent threat from the very first session. The Surge is not a one-time event — it is the first demonstration of a recurring danger that only escalates as seals degrade over time.

### The Food Clock

NYC has 8-10 days of rationed food at 800 calories per day. By Day 5-6 the crisis becomes apparent to the general population. By Day 8 the first food riots break out. By Day 10-12 a starvation spiral begins unless Amundi trade has already started producing results. Ammunition is finite and will be exhausted for routine security use by approximately Month 4, which further constrains the ability to maintain order as food pressure mounts.

### The Verdancy's Arrival

Spore-drifts cross the western passes on Days 3-4. Carnivorous Verdancy creatures appear by Day 6. The western front activates whether or not NYC is ready, forcing an immediate two-front strategic reality: galleries below, Verdancy to the west.

### The Undead's Interest

The Still Cities observe NYC from Gebed Mur. They make contact early in the timeline, though the exact timing depends on which house acts first. The undead are not an immediate military threat but rather a diplomatic and intelligence variable that shapes NYC's strategic options from the first week onward.

### The Five Revelations (Days 3-7)

These revelations cascade through the population in roughly this order, each one destabilizing assumptions that the previous social order depended on:

1. **World Outside** — exploration parties confirm this is not Earth; they find human civilizations with pre-existing cultures, economies, and power structures
2. **Magic Is Real** — Legacy-activated abilities manifest visibly among a small percentage of the population, creating an immediate new axis of social stratification
3. **The Food Clock** — rationing begins; the moral economy supersedes pre-transport economic and political structures as survival becomes the organizing principle
4. **Government Cannot Help** — communication lag and infrastructure collapse make centralized governance impossible; authority devolves to whoever can organize locally
5. **The Dead Are Still Here** — spontaneous revenants rise from unburied dead, forcing mandatory cremation protocols and confronting the population with the supernatural reality of their new world

## Medium-Term Pressures (Months 1-6)

- **Borough divergence** — distinct geographic fronts (galleries, western Verdancy, eastern trade routes, northern wilderness) pull the boroughs toward functional autonomy, each developing its own priorities and power structures
- **Trade dependency** — NYC cannot feed itself at any population level above roughly 100,000. Amundi trade is not a temporary bridge but a permanent structural requirement, giving Amundi leverage that only grows over time
- **Gallery economic development** — the depth-danger-wealth spiral is the central strategic tension of NYC's economy. Deeper gallery levels contain greater resources but also stronger servitors and accelerate seal degradation
- **Subterranean river** — if the underground river system can be cleared and secured, it transforms Manhattan's political and economic position by providing reliable fresh water and an internal transport network

## Long-Term Pressures (Months 6+)

- **Pelegrinian encirclement** — escalates through a predictable sequence: assessment, economic pressure, diplomatic isolation, and finally confrontation. The pace depends on how visibly NYC accumulates Outsider artifacts and gallery wealth
- **Sarxian shadow** — intelligence flows through Qasir to Sarx; the accumulation of Outsider artifacts accelerates the timeline toward direct Sarxian interest and intervention
- **The depth question** — deeper gallery exploration weakens the Imperator's cage and triggers adaptation in servitor types and tactics. Every resource gain from the galleries carries a long-term security cost
- **Verdancy's advance** — without magical countermeasures, the western valleys are slowly lost to Verdancy expansion, compressing NYC's usable territory and increasing population density pressure

## The First Thirty Days (Reference Timeline)

- **Hours 1-6**: Transport shock, technology failure, mass entrapment (subway, elevators), roughly 30% of the population in acute shock
- **Day 1**: Imperator surge, JDA formation, armed geography crystallizes into four zone types — JDA-controlled zones, neighborhood-defense zones, criminal-controlled zones, and uncontrolled zones
- **Days 3-7**: The Five Revelations cascade through the population; exploration parties return with confirmation of the world outside; magic manifests among Legacy-bearers
- **Days 7-14**: The starvation window opens and closes depending on Amundi trade success; first significant emigration begins as some residents leave for Latter Earth settlements
- **Days 14-30**: Borough federation crystallizes into a working (if fragile) political structure; gallery economy completes its first extraction-and-trade cycle; the western front hardens into a defensible line; undead negotiation channels open
