# Greypeak

## §history
Greypeak is a monastic warrior state founded by veterans of a forgotten war. A century and a half ago, a company of soldiers from a dozen nations was hired to clear a Legacy ruin in the mountains. Whatever they found inside changed them. They refused to discuss the experience, settled in the surrounding valleys, and established a community dedicated to martial perfection and spiritual discipline. Their descendants maintain the same traditions, training ceaselessly for a conflict they say is coming but will not describe.

## §geography
High mountain valleys in a remote section of the Spine of the World. The terrain is severe — sharp peaks, narrow passes, and alpine meadows accessible only by difficult trails. The climate is cold, with heavy snowfall and brief summers. The Legacy ruin that the founders explored sits at the center of the territory, sealed and guarded by the Order of the Grey Peak. Training grounds and monasteries dot the valleys.

## §government
A martial order. The Master of the Peak holds authority, selected through a combination of combat trials and meditation. Below the Master, a council of Seniors — veteran warriors who have completed all levels of training — governs daily affairs. All citizens are members of the Order from birth; those who choose not to train may leave freely but may not return.

## §culture
Greypeak culture is austere, disciplined, and intensely focused. Every activity — eating, walking, conversation — is treated as a form of training. Physical conditioning and combat skill are expected of everyone. Meditation and philosophical study balance the martial focus. Art is calligraphic: precise, minimal, and meaningful. Food is simple and nutritious. Leisure is nearly nonexistent; rest is a form of training.

## §cultural-implications
Combat and discipline skill checks are paramount. Greypeak NPCs assess everyone as a potential combatant. Displays of martial skill earn immediate respect. Laziness and indulgence provoke contempt. PCs willing to train alongside the Order gain access to exceptional instruction. Those seeking to learn must demonstrate sincerity through action, not words.

## §sensory-palette
Greypeak is sharp and clean — cold mountain air, the crack of wooden practice weapons, bare stone training halls. Snow covers the peaks year-round. The monasteries are austere: grey stone, minimal furnishing, everything in its place. The sound of synchronized breathing during group meditation carries across the valleys at dawn. There is no clutter, no excess, no wasted anything.

## §voice-notes
Greypeakers speak in short, precise sentences. They waste no words. Common expressions: "The body remembers" and "Prepare." They ask direct questions and expect direct answers. Long explanations are cut short with "Shorter." They communicate as much through stance and gesture as through speech.

## §adventure-hooks
- The sealed Legacy ruin has begun emitting a signal. The Order's founders left instructions: when the signal comes, the Order must enter. The current Master is preparing, but the instructions also warn that not all who enter will return.
- A foreign power has discovered Greypeak's location and sent agents to learn its fighting techniques. The Order has detected the infiltration and wants the agents found without revealing the breach to the general population.
- A young trainee has spontaneously manifested abilities that the founders described in their sealed writings — abilities they said would mark the beginning of what they trained for.
- The Master of the Peak has died unexpectedly. The succession trials have revealed that no living member of the Order can pass the final test. The Order faces its first crisis of leadership.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — What the founders found in the Legacy ruin was not a threat but a message — a warning about something that will arrive from outside the known world. The sealed writings describe it in terms that do not correspond to any known phenomenon. The Order's entire existence is preparation for this event. When it will come, and whether the Order's training will be sufficient, is unknown.
