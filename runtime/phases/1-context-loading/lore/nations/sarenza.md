# Sarenza

## §history
Sarenza is a nation of poets, philosophers, and assassins. Built around the shores of the Jade Gulf, it was founded when a consortium of city-states formalized their ancient trade and cultural ties into a single polity. The union was driven not by war but by a shared cultural identity so strong that political separation had become absurd. Sarenza has since become the Latter Earth's center for learning, medicine, and — less openly acknowledged — the most sophisticated intelligence network in the known world.

## §geography
A sun-drenched coastal region along the Jade Gulf, with olive groves, citrus orchards, and terraced hillsides overlooking turquoise waters. The climate is warm and dry in summer, mild and rainy in winter. White-walled cities cascade down hillsides to busy harbors. Inland, the terrain rises to rocky uplands dotted with ancient ruins. The jade-tinted waters of the gulf give everything a distinctive green cast.

## §government
A federation of city-states, each governed by a council of notables — merchants, scholars, and guild masters. A federal senate handles shared concerns. The intelligence service — the Velvet Office — operates independently of all formal government structures and answers to no one publicly. Everyone knows it exists; no one can prove anything specific.

## §culture
Sareni culture prizes beauty, intellect, and subtlety. Education is universal and excellent. The arts flourish — music, poetry, painting, and architecture of extraordinary refinement. Medicine is the most advanced in the known world. Beneath the gracious surface, every interaction carries layers of meaning. Sareni rarely say what they mean or mean what they say, and they consider this a virtue, not a flaw.

## §cultural-implications
Intelligence, perception, and social skill checks are paramount. Sareni NPCs communicate through implication and nuance — PCs who miss subtleties will miss critical information. Medical knowledge is respected. Direct confrontation is gauche. The Velvet Office may take interest in PCs who attract attention.

## §sensory-palette
Sarenza smells of lemon groves, salt air, and the warm stone of ancient buildings baking in the sun. Music drifts from open windows — lute and voice in haunting minor keys. The light is golden and sharp, casting deep shadows in narrow streets. Markets display pyramids of colorful fruit and stacks of leather-bound books in equal measure.

## §voice-notes
Sareni speak in musical, flowing sentences, often leaving thoughts deliberately unfinished: "One might consider..." trailing into a meaningful look. Common expressions: "The gulf keeps its secrets, and so do we" and "Beauty is truth's only honest face." They are charming, warm, and fundamentally unknowable.

## §adventure-hooks
- A prominent philosopher has published a treatise that appears to contain coded instructions for activating a Legacy weapon. She claims it is purely theoretical. The Velvet Office disagrees.
- A plague has struck one of the gulf cities. Sareni medicine cannot identify it. The symptoms match no known disease — they match a Third Age weapon described in ancient texts.
- A master assassin of the Velvet Office has defected to a foreign power, carrying a decade of intelligence. The Office wants them silenced before they can talk.
- An underwater archaeological expedition in the Jade Gulf has discovered a perfectly preserved Legacy city on the seabed. The discovery has been classified, but rumors are spreading.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The Velvet Office's true purpose may extend beyond conventional espionage. Certain operations, visible only in fragmentary records, suggest the Office has been pursuing a single, long-term objective for its entire existence. What that objective is — and who, if anyone, originally set it — remains the deepest mystery of Sareni politics.
