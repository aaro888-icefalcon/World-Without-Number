# Chorath

## §history
Chorath is a theocratic state founded two centuries ago by the prophet Choreth, who claimed divine visions from a being imprisoned beneath the salt flats. The faith spread rapidly among the desert peoples, and within a generation Chorath had unified the scattered oasis settlements into a single polity. The original prophet's teachings emphasized communal ownership and spiritual discipline, though successive high priests have interpreted these principles with increasing flexibility.

## §geography
Chorath occupies a vast expanse of salt flats, rocky desert, and scattered oases along the southern edge of the Interior Wastes. Temperatures are extreme — scorching days and freezing nights. Water is the most precious resource. The salt flats themselves are the remnants of an ancient sea, drained by [UNKNOWN] means during a prior age.

## §government
A theocracy ruled by the High Priest of the Imprisoned Voice, advised by a council of twelve temple elders. Secular administration is handled by temple-appointed governors. Religious law and civil law are identical. Heresy is the most serious crime.

## §culture
Chorathi culture revolves around faith, water conservation, and communal survival. Generosity with water is the highest virtue. Art takes the form of intricate sand mandalas and devotional chanting. Gender roles are egalitarian — the Imprisoned Voice does not distinguish between its children. Asceticism is admired but not required.

## §cultural-implications
Religious knowledge and sincere piety grant social advantages. Blasphemy or disrespect toward the Imprisoned Voice provokes immediate hostility. Water-sharing rituals are important social bonding — refusing offered water is a grave insult.

## §sensory-palette
Chorath is a land of blinding white salt under a merciless sun. The air tastes of mineral dust and dried herbs. At dawn and dusk, the salt flats reflect the sky in shimmering pinks and golds. The chanting of the faithful drifts from every temple, low and rhythmic, echoing off adobe walls.

## §voice-notes
Chorathi speak with a melodic cadence influenced by devotional chanting. They frequently invoke blessings: "May the Voice shelter you" and "By the salt and the silence." They pause before answering questions, as hasty speech is considered spiritually careless.

## §adventure-hooks
- The salt flats have begun to crack open in places, revealing dark caverns beneath. Something is moving down there.
- A splinter sect claims the High Priest has been corrupted and the true teachings of Choreth have been suppressed. They are gathering followers in the outer oases.
- A trade caravan carrying vital supplies has vanished in the deep desert. The only clue is a single surviving camel, its saddle covered in an unidentifiable crystalline residue.
- The Imprisoned Voice has fallen silent for the first time in two centuries. The High Priest is concealing this fact.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The nature of the Imprisoned Voice. Is it a god, a Legacy artificial intelligence, a trapped Sorcerer-King, or something else entirely? The deepest chambers beneath the salt flats are sealed and forbidden. What Choreth actually found there has never been disclosed.
