# Lumengarde

## §history
Lumengarde is the Latter Earth's foremost center of magical education. Founded two centuries ago by a consortium of mages who believed that magical knowledge should be organized, preserved, and taught systematically rather than hoarded by individual practitioners, the Arcane University at Lumengarde has trained generations of spellcasters. The nation that grew around the university exists primarily to support it. Lumengarde is neutral in all conflicts — a pragmatic necessity, since every nation wants access to its graduates.

## §geography
A city-state built around the Arcane University campus, situated on a convergence of ley lines — channels of residual magical energy from prior ages. The campus is a sprawling complex of towers, libraries, laboratories, and practice grounds. The surrounding city provides housing, commerce, and services. The terrain is gently hilly with pleasant meadows. The climate is unnaturally stable — the ley line convergence moderates weather. Magical side-effects are common: flowers that bloom in impossible colors, stones that hum, water that flows uphill in places.

## §government
An academic council. The Archmage presides; department heads form the council. The university governs the city through appointed administrators. Citizenship is open but arcane education is mandatory for political participation. Non-magical residents have full civil rights but no vote. Town-gown tensions exist but are managed through generous employment of non-magical staff.

## §culture
Lumengarde culture is intellectual, competitive, and obsessed with magical theory. Status derives from academic achievement and magical power. Debates between schools of magical thought are the primary entertainment. Art explores the intersection of magic and aesthetics — living sculptures, enchanted music, illusory theater. Food is magically enhanced — consistently excellent but sometimes unsettlingly novel. Students from every nation create a cosmopolitan atmosphere.

## §cultural-implications
Arcane knowledge and intellectual skill checks are paramount. Lumengarde NPCs respect magical aptitude and theoretical knowledge. Non-magical PCs may face condescension but can earn respect through sharp intellect. Academic rivalries are intense — PCs may be recruited as pawns in factional disputes. The university's library is the best in the world, but access is restricted and political.

## §sensory-palette
Lumengarde shimmers. Magical energy is visible to the trained eye — faint aurora-like ripples in the air, motes of light drifting between buildings. The towers of the university glow with different colored lights representing different schools of magic. The air tastes faintly of ozone and smells of old books and reagents. Strange sounds — chimes, whispers, the crack of discharged spells — echo across campus at all hours.

## §voice-notes
Lumengardians speak with precise, academic diction, using technical magical terminology in casual conversation. They cite sources: "According to Pelwyn's Third Theorem..." Common expressions: "Theoretically sound" (approval) and "Insufficient evidence" (dismissal). They argue for sport and take academic disagreement personally. Magical jargon is impenetrable to outsiders.

## §adventure-hooks
- A magical experiment has gone wrong, creating a pocket of altered reality on campus. Inside, the laws of physics are different. Students are trapped within, and conventional magic cannot breach the boundary.
- The university library's restricted section has been robbed. The stolen texts contain information on a Third Age weapon. The thief left no magical trace — an impossibility within the university's detection wards.
- A ley line has shifted course, threatening to destabilize the convergence that powers the university. The cause appears to be a Legacy device activating somewhere deep underground.
- A visiting scholar has published a paper proving that all current magical traditions are derived from a single, lost system of far greater power. The paper includes enough practical detail to be dangerous, and copies are spreading beyond the university's control.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The ley line convergence beneath Lumengarde is not natural. It was engineered — the ley lines were deliberately routed to this point during a prior age. The university was built here because of the convergence, but the convergence exists because of something buried at its center. What that something is, and whether the university's magical activities are powering it, depleting it, or waking it up, is a question the Archmage has been investigating in secret.
