# Kaelundra

## §history
Kaelundra is a forest nation — not a nation in a forest, but a nation that is the forest. The Kaelundri live in symbiosis with an enormous old-growth woodland that they believe is a single living organism, connected at the root level by Legacy bio-engineering from the Second Age. The trees respond to Kaelundri presence, providing shelter, food, and defense. In return, the Kaelundri tend, protect, and expand the forest. This relationship has persisted for centuries, and the Kaelundri cannot imagine existence apart from it.

## §geography
A vast, ancient forest covering an area larger than most kingdoms. The trees are enormous — some over three hundred feet tall, with trunks wide enough to house families. The canopy is so dense that the forest floor exists in permanent twilight. Bioluminescent fungi and plants provide light. Rivers and streams run beneath the canopy. The climate is temperate and moist, moderated by the forest itself. The forest's edge is abrupt — dense woodland gives way to open land within a few dozen yards.

## §government
A council of Grove-Speakers — individuals who can communicate with the forest through physical contact with the root network. The Eldest Speaker leads, though the forest itself influences decisions through the information it provides. Governance is communal and consensus-based. The forest's wellbeing is the highest priority — all other concerns are subordinate.

## §culture
Kaelundri culture is organic, communal, and deeply reverent of the forest. They live in tree-houses, wear clothing of woven bark and leaf, and eat what the forest provides — fruit, nuts, fungi, and the honey of giant bees that inhabit the upper canopy. Art is living — shaped trees, trained vines, bioluminescent gardens. Music uses wooden instruments and forest acoustics to create sounds that carry for miles. Waste is returned to the forest; nothing is discarded.

## §cultural-implications
Nature, survival, and perception skill checks are valued. Kaelundri NPCs are welcoming to those who show respect for the forest and hostile to those who damage it. Carrying axes or fire into the forest is a grave offense. PCs with druidic or nature-based abilities are treated as honored guests. Knowledge of Legacy bio-engineering earns special trust.

## §sensory-palette
Kaelundra is alive in every direction — the creak of ancient wood, the rustle of leaves in a wind that never reaches the ground, the soft glow of bioluminescent fungi casting blue-green light on moss-covered roots. The air is thick with the scent of earth, sap, and flowering vines. Birdsong and insect hum create a constant ambient chord. Everything grows, moves, breathes.

## §voice-notes
Kaelundri speak softly, with a rhythm that mirrors the forest's own sounds — rising and falling like wind through branches. They use organic metaphors: "That idea has strong roots" and "Let it grow before you harvest." Common greeting: "The forest sees you." They touch trees when speaking, as though consulting. Long silences are comfortable — they are listening to the forest.

## §adventure-hooks
- The forest is dying in one sector — trees withering from the roots up. The Grove-Speakers cannot identify the cause. Something underground is poisoning the root network.
- A lumber company from a neighboring nation has begun illegal logging at the forest's edge. The forest is responding — aggressively. Kaelundri diplomacy has failed, and the forest's defensive reactions are escalating beyond Kaelundri control.
- A Grove-Speaker has entered a deep communion with the forest and not returned to normal consciousness. She speaks, but the words are not hers — they are the forest's, and they describe memories from the Second Age.
- The forest has begun growing in a new direction, rapidly, engulfing farmland. The Kaelundri insist they are not directing it. The forest appears to be reaching toward something — a Legacy structure buried beneath the plains.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The forest is not merely alive — it is intelligent, in a way that defies human understanding. The Grove-Speakers access only a fraction of its awareness. At the deepest level of communion, Speakers report encountering a presence — vast, ancient, and patient. Whether this presence is the forest itself, something that inhabits the forest, or something the forest was designed to contain is the deepest mystery of Kaelundra.
