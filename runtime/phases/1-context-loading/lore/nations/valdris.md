# Valdris

## §history
Valdris is a military dictatorship forged from necessity. A century ago, the region was overrun by creatures from a breached Legacy vault — an event called the Crimson Flood. The civilian government collapsed. A general named Aldric Valdris imposed martial law, organized the defense, and drove the creatures back over eighteen months of brutal fighting. The emergency powers were never rescinded. Valdris is now a garrison state, perpetually prepared for a threat that may or may not return. The current dictator, General Aldric IV, is the founder's great-granddaughter.

## §geography
A defensible plateau bordered by deep river gorges on three sides and a mountain range on the fourth. The terrain is rolling grassland with scattered fortifications. The climate is continental — hot summers and cold winters. The sealed Legacy vault lies at the plateau's center, surrounded by a permanent exclusion zone patrolled by the military. The soil is fertile but agriculture is secondary to military preparedness.

## §government
A military dictatorship. The Supreme Commander holds absolute authority. The officer corps forms the ruling class. All citizens serve mandatory military terms. Civilian administration exists but is subordinate to military command. Dissent is not tolerated. The state is efficient, well-organized, and deeply oppressive.

## §culture
Valdrian culture is martial, disciplined, and haunted by the Crimson Flood. Duty, sacrifice, and obedience are the cardinal virtues. Art is functional — patriotic songs, memorial architecture, propaganda murals. Entertainment is athletic competition and war games. Food is standardized and nutritious but flavorless. The population is psychologically shaped by constant threat awareness — anxiety is endemic but unacknowledged.

## §cultural-implications
Military bearing and discipline earn respect. PCs who follow orders and demonstrate combat readiness are treated well. Questioning authority provokes suspicion. The secret police (the Watchers) monitor foreigners closely. PCs with Legacy knowledge may attract unwanted military interest.

## §sensory-palette
Valdris is orderly and tense. Fortifications dominate every skyline. The sound of marching boots and drill commands echoes through clean, geometric cities. The air smells of gun oil, leather, and the metallic tang of the exclusion zone's boundary markers. At night, searchlights sweep the perimeter of the sealed vault, and the distant whine of Legacy containment systems is audible in the silence.

## §voice-notes
Valdrians speak in short, efficient sentences. They use military terminology in daily speech: "Acknowledged," "Negative," "Situation assessed." Common expressions: "The Flood remembers even if we forget" and "Vigilance is the price of survival." They do not make small talk. Silence is comfortable; idle chatter is suspect.

## §adventure-hooks
- The Legacy vault's containment systems are showing signs of degradation. The military needs specialists to assess the situation, but admitting weakness is politically impossible. They are hiring outsiders through intermediaries.
- A dissident movement is growing in the lower ranks. Their leader claims the Crimson Flood was not an accident — it was deliberately triggered by the first Valdris to justify seizing power.
- Creatures matching Crimson Flood descriptions have been sighted outside Valdrian territory for the first time. Either the vault is leaking, or there is a second source.
- General Aldric IV has been behaving erratically. Her inner circle is concerned. She has been spending hours alone in a chamber near the vault's perimeter, speaking to something no one else can see.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The Legacy vault is not fully sealed. A narrow passage remains open — deliberately — and the Valdris family has used it every generation. What they retrieve from inside, what they leave behind, and what agreement they have with whatever inhabits the vault is the nation's darkest secret.
