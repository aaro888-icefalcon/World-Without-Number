# Reikmarsh

## §history
Reikmarsh is a confederation of free cities built in the vast marshlands where the Varenthar delta meets the Sunward Sea. The marshes were considered uninhabitable until resourceful refugees, outcasts, and fugitives discovered that the wetlands offered both protection and unexpected bounty. Over a century, the stilt-cities grew from shanty towns into prosperous trading posts. Reikmarsh declared independence from the surrounding kingdoms fifty years ago, and its impassable terrain has made the declaration stick.

## §geography
A vast river delta of channels, mudflats, mangrove forests, and reed beds. The land is barely above sea level — during storm surges, it is below it. Cities are built on stilts, artificial islands, and repurposed Legacy platforms found in the marshes. The climate is warm and humid. Mosquitoes are endemic. Navigation requires expert knowledge of constantly shifting channels.

## §government
A loose confederation of city councils. Each stilt-city governs itself through elected aldermen. A confederal assembly meets quarterly to address shared concerns — defense, trade agreements, waterway maintenance. There is no central executive. Disputes are settled by negotiation or, failing that, trade embargo.

## §culture
Reikmarshers are independent, resourceful, and deeply suspicious of authority. They value self-reliance and ingenuity. The arts favor boat-building, weaving, and a distinctive form of call-and-response singing carried across the water. Food is fish, rice, and swamp vegetables prepared with fiery spices. Social bonds are formed through shared labor — raising a building or clearing a channel together creates lasting trust.

## §cultural-implications
Survival, navigation, and crafting skill checks are valued. Reikmarsh NPCs distrust titles and credentials — they judge by what you can do, not who you claim to be. Offering to help with physical labor is the fastest way to earn trust. Attempts to impose authority are met with passive resistance or outright defiance.

## §sensory-palette
Reikmarsh is wet. The air is thick with humidity, smelling of salt, mud, and blooming water lilies. Wooden walkways creak over dark water. The constant chorus of frogs, insects, and wading birds fills every silence. Mist rises at dawn, turning the stilt-cities into ghostly silhouettes. Cooking smoke from a thousand galley-kitchens drifts between the houses.

## §voice-notes
Reikmarshers speak in a rapid, clipped dialect with many nautical terms repurposed for daily life. They call problems "leaks" and solutions "patches." Common expressions: "The marsh provides" and "Still water hides deep mud." They communicate across distances by whistling — a complex signaling system outsiders find impenetrable.

## §adventure-hooks
- A new island has risen from the marsh overnight — solid stone, dry, and covered in Legacy inscriptions. Multiple cities are claiming it, and tensions are rising.
- Something is hunting in the deep channels. Boats are being found empty, their crews gone without signs of struggle. The attacks are moving closer to the populated areas.
- A Legacy platform that serves as the foundation for one of the largest cities has begun to sink. Engineers suspect it is being pulled downward by something beneath the mud.
- Smugglers are running a Legacy artifact pipeline through the marshes. The artifacts are heading to a buyer whose identity is carefully concealed.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The marshes contain Legacy structures that are partially submerged and extend deep into the delta mud. The Reikmarshers have built atop them for generations without fully exploring them. Occasionally, lights are visible deep underwater, and the Legacy platforms emit vibrations during certain tidal conditions. What lies beneath the marsh is the confederation's collective blind spot — they depend on it but dare not investigate.
