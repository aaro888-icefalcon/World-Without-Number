# Kethraza

## §geography
Kethraza stretches along a narrow coastal strip between the Spine Mountains and the Ember Sea, a volcanic coastline of black sand beaches, steaming hot springs, and terraced cliffs. The climate is warm and humid, moderated by ocean breezes. Volcanic soil makes the terraced hillsides extraordinarily fertile, supporting dense cultivation of spices, coffee, and tropical fruit.

## §government
An elected monarchy in which the ruler, called the Ashen Throne, is chosen every twelve years by a vote of all adult citizens. Candidates must pass trials of wisdom, combat, and governance before standing for election. The Ashen Throne wields broad executive power but is advised by a permanent bureaucracy of scribes who maintain continuity between reigns.

## §culture
Kethrazi culture celebrates competition and individual excellence. Public contests in athletics, poetry, debate, and craftsmanship are constant, and victory in these competitions confers genuine social status. Despite the competitive spirit, Kethrazi maintain strong communal bonds; the same person you defeat in a poetry contest may save your life in a volcanic emergency. Hospitality is generous, and feasting is the primary social ritual.

## §economy
Kethraza's spice trade is legendary, supplying rare volcanic-soil cultivars that grow nowhere else. Obsidian quarrying and the export of geothermally heated mineral salts supplement agricultural wealth. The nation's hot springs attract visitors from across the Latter Earth, and a thriving hospitality industry has grown around the most spectacular thermal sites.

## §military
Kethrazi maintain a professional standing army built around heavy infantry equipped with obsidian-edged weapons and volcanic-glass armor plates. The coastal fortifications incorporate natural lava tubes and geothermal vents as defensive features. A small but aggressive navy patrols the Ember Sea, protecting trade routes from pirates and rival powers.

## §religion
Kethrazi worship centers on the Ember Court, a pantheon of volcanic spirits believed to inhabit the mountains. Eruptions are interpreted as divine communication, and a priestly order of volcanologists maintains observatories on every active peak. The most sacred ritual involves descending into a dormant caldera to retrieve cooled lava stones, which are carved into personal talismans.

## §history
Kethraza was founded by a coalition of fishing villages that banded together after a catastrophic eruption destroyed a previous civilization on the same coast. The survivors vowed to never again be caught unprepared, establishing the volcanic observatory network and the tradition of competitive governance that ensures only the most capable lead. A century ago, Kethraza fought a brief but decisive naval war with Blackport over shipping rights in the Ember Sea.
