# Zardath

## §history
Zardath is a cursed land — or so its neighbors believe. Once a prosperous kingdom, it was devastated two centuries ago when a Legacy weapon detonated beneath its capital, releasing a wave of mutagenic energy that transformed the land and its people. The survivors adapted, developing resistance to the contamination and eventually embracing their altered state. Zardath is now a nation of the changed — people who bear visible mutations but retain their humanity, their culture, and their fierce determination to survive in a world that fears them.

## §geography
A blighted landscape of twisted forests, iridescent bogs, and terrain warped by residual mutagenic energy. The contamination is not uniform — some areas are nearly normal, while others are lethal to unprotected visitors. The capital was rebuilt in a relatively stable zone. Rivers glow faintly at night. The climate would be temperate if not for the contamination's effect on weather patterns, which produces unpredictable storms and temperature swings.

## §government
A council of Elders — the oldest and most heavily mutated citizens, who have survived the contamination longest and understand it best. The council governs by consensus and prioritizes survival above all else. Military defense is organized by a war-leader elected from the most capable fighters. Diplomacy is handled by the least visibly mutated citizens, for pragmatic reasons.

## §culture
Zardathi culture is resilient, practical, and defiantly proud. They do not hide their mutations or apologize for them. Community bonds are exceptionally strong — shared suffering and external hostility have forged an unbreakable solidarity. Art celebrates transformation and adaptation. Music features haunting harmonics that unmutated vocal cords cannot produce. Outsiders who show respect are welcomed; those who show revulsion are expelled.

## §cultural-implications
Survival and adaptation skill checks are valued. Zardathi NPCs assess outsiders primarily on their reaction to the mutations — acceptance opens all doors, disgust closes them permanently. Knowledge of Legacy contamination and mutation is deeply respected. PCs who are themselves unusual or marked are treated as kin.

## §sensory-palette
Zardath is eerily beautiful. Bioluminescent fungi glow blue and green in the twisted forests. The air carries a faint metallic sweetness. The sky sometimes shimmers with aurora-like effects even during the day. Animals are strange — too many legs, too many eyes, but alive and adapted. The settlements are colorful and defiant, painted in bright hues against the strangeness of the landscape.

## §voice-notes
Zardathi speak with unusual vocal textures — harmonics, resonances, and timbres that reflect their mutations. They are direct and unpretentious. Common expressions: "We bend, we do not break" and "The changed world is still the world." They use "cousin" as a term of friendship with non-Zardathi who have proven themselves. They laugh often — survival humor runs deep.

## §adventure-hooks
- The mutagenic contamination is expanding. The boundary that has been stable for decades is moving outward. The Elders need to know why and how to stop it.
- A foreign alchemist claims she can reverse Zardathi mutations. Some citizens want the cure; others see it as an attack on their identity. The political division threatens to tear the nation apart.
- A Legacy installation at the contamination's epicenter has become accessible for the first time. It may contain the weapon that caused the disaster — or the means to control it.
- Children are being born in Zardath without visible mutations for the first time in generations. The Elders are not celebrating — they are terrified. In their experience, the contamination does not simply stop.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The mutations are not random. Over generations, they have followed a pattern — a progression toward something. The Elders have tracked this progression in secret records and believe the contamination is not destroying the Zardathi but transforming them toward a specific end state designed by whoever created the Legacy weapon. What that end state is, and whether it is salvation or something else, is the nation's most terrifying question.
