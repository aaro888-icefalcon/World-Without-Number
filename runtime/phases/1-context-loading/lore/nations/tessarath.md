# Tessarath

## §geography
Tessarath sprawls across a vast arid plateau ringed by eroded sandstone mesas and cut by deep slot canyons. The climate is hot and dry, with brief violent rainy seasons that transform dry wadis into torrents. Oases dot the plateau, each supporting a settlement, and an ancient Legacy aqueduct system still channels water through underground channels across hundreds of miles.

## §government
A theocratic confederation ruled by the Sunsworn Council, a body of priest-governors who each administer one of the major oasis-cities. The High Sunsworn is elected from among the council members upon the death of the predecessor through a ritual process involving fasting, divination, and — allegedly — direct communion with their solar deity.

## §culture
Tessarathi culture revolves around water discipline and solar worship. Wasting water is the gravest social offense. Hospitality requires offering water before any other greeting. Architecture is designed to channel shade and airflow. Scholarship is highly valued, and each oasis-city maintains a library carved into the cool stone of its canyon walls.

## §economy
Tessarath controls critical overland trade routes connecting the eastern and western nations, and caravan taxes form the backbone of the economy. The plateau yields copper, turquoise, and a rare crystallized mineral called sunite, which glows faintly and is used in Legacy-derived lighting systems. Textile weaving, particularly silk-fine cloth from desert moth cocoons, is a major export.

## §military
Tessarathi field light cavalry mounted on desert-bred horses famed for endurance, supported by canyon-fortress garrisons that are nearly impossible to assault. The Sunsworn maintain an elite temple guard, the Burning Lance, whose members undergo rigorous martial and spiritual training. Foreign armies that enter the plateau rarely leave it.

## §religion
Solar worship dominates Tessarathi life. The sun is understood as the Awakened Eye, a conscious force that scours corruption and reveals truth. Dawn and dusk prayers are universal. Eclipses are treated as sacred events requiring total silence. Temples are open-roofed, with polished metal reflectors that channel sunlight into subterranean chambers.

## §history
Tessarath coalesced from scattered oasis communities when the Legacy aqueduct system was rediscovered and restored three centuries ago. The priest who led the restoration, Aveth Sunkeeper, became the first High Sunsworn and established the theocratic system that endures today. A century ago, Tessarath repelled a major invasion from the north, trapping an entire army in the canyon systems.

## §current-affairs
The Legacy aqueduct system has begun behaving erratically, delivering water to oases that were previously dry while reducing flow to established cities. The Sunsworn Council is divided over whether this is a mechanical failure or a deliberate redirection.
