# Amarantine

## §history
Amarantine is a city-state of immortals — or rather, of people who age at a fraction of the normal rate. The city was built around a Legacy fountain whose waters dramatically slow aging. Discovered four centuries ago, the fountain transformed a minor settlement into one of the most coveted places in the Latter Earth. The original settlers and their descendants — the Unfaded — have accumulated centuries of knowledge, wealth, and political power. Immigration is tightly controlled: the fountain's output is limited, and every new resident reduces the share available to existing ones.

## §geography
A walled city-state built into a mountainside around the Legacy fountain, which flows from a cave system deep within the rock. The surrounding territory is a narrow but fertile valley with terraced gardens and orchards. The climate is mild year-round — possibly influenced by the fountain's Legacy systems. The valley is enclosed by steep mountains with only two passes, both heavily fortified.

## §government
A gerontocracy in the most literal sense. The Council of the Unfaded — the oldest living residents, some over three centuries old — governs with the patience and long-term thinking that only extreme longevity permits. Laws change slowly. Policies are measured in decades. Short-term thinking is considered a character flaw.

## §culture
Amarantine culture is refined, patient, and deeply conservative. When you might live for centuries, reputation matters enormously and grudges last lifetimes. Art is executed with painstaking perfection over years or decades. Music favors complex compositions that unfold over hours. Relationships are intricate and layered with centuries of history. Outsiders find the pace maddening; residents find outsiders recklessly hasty.

## §cultural-implications
Patience and long-term thinking earn respect. Amarantine NPCs speak and act on timescales that frustrate normal mortals. Rushing them is counterproductive. Historical knowledge — especially personal memory of past events — is the highest social currency. PCs who offer genuinely novel information or perspectives are valued, as novelty is rare when you have lived for centuries.

## §sensory-palette
Amarantine is timeless — literally. Architecture spans centuries of styles, layered and interwoven. Gardens are ancient, their trees enormous, their flower beds perfect through generations of tending. The air smells of old stone, perpetual bloom, and the faint mineral sweetness of the fountain's water. Silence predominates; the residents have long since outgrown the need for unnecessary noise.

## §voice-notes
Amarantines speak with unhurried precision, as though each word were chosen from a vast internal library. They reference events centuries past as though they happened yesterday. Common expressions: "In time" and "I recall when that was different." They rarely ask questions — they usually already know the answer. They address newcomers with gentle condescension.

## §adventure-hooks
- The fountain's flow has diminished for the first time in recorded history. The Unfaded are aging — slowly, but visibly. Panic lurks beneath the city's placid surface.
- An Unfaded council member has been murdered — the first murder in Amarantine in two centuries. The motive appears to involve a grudge that originated three hundred years ago.
- A group of outsiders has discovered a way to synthesize the fountain's water. The Unfaded see this as an existential threat to their monopoly and their society.
- The cave system beneath the fountain has shifted, revealing new passages. Exploration teams have found evidence that the fountain is not a natural phenomenon but a component of a much larger Legacy system — one that may have a purpose beyond granting longevity.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The fountain does not merely slow aging. The Unfaded who have lived longest — those over four centuries — are beginning to change in subtle ways. Their senses sharpen. Their need for sleep diminishes. Some report hearing things at frequencies normal ears cannot detect. Whether the fountain is a gift, an experiment, or the first stage of a transformation is a question the oldest Unfaded discuss only among themselves.
