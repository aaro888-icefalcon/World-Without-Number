# The Verdancy

## §history

The Verdancy has been expanding for centuries, a continuously ongoing ecological catastrophe powered by ancient Workings housed within the Suldant cathedral-cities. Its origins are tied to the Suldant — beings created or transformed by those ancient Workings into immortal, intelligent plant-intelligences. Over the ages the forest has consumed entire regions, and every attempt to permanently halt its growth has failed without reaching the cathedral-cities themselves. Thirty-six or more attempts at organized counter-offensives have been mounted across recorded history; none penetrated deep enough to disrupt the core Workings.

## §geography

Not a nation but a lethal ecological zone. The Verdancy expands wherever growth is possible, covering vast tracts of land in polychrome vegetation — greens, purples, reds, bioluminescent blues. The edge currently sits 3-5 miles from NYC's western passes. The cathedral-cities are massive structures deep within the forest that generate the expansion-driving magic. The terrain is a shifting, living maze of root systems, canopy layers, and fungal networks. No stable paths exist; the forest rearranges itself over days or weeks.

## §government

No government exists. The Suldant are the closest thing to rulers — each cathedral-city houses its own Suldant intelligence with independent goals. They cooperate tactically through root networks and chemical signaling but do not maintain a unified strategy. Different Suldant pursue different approaches: some are conservative, consolidating existing growth; others are aggressive, pushing expansion at maximum speed; still others are experimental, breeding novel creature types and testing new strategies against human defenses.

## §culture

Alien. The Verdancy's "culture" is growth, consumption, adaptation. The Suldant communicate through root networks and chemical signaling. They can speak to humans but think in utterly non-human terms — growth is good, stasis is death, the distinction between predator and prey is irrelevant. There is no art, no commerce, no diplomacy in any human sense. The closest analogue is an ecosystem with semi-coordinated apex intelligences.

Incursion pattern against NYC: minor probes weekly, significant pushes monthly, siege-scale assaults every 2-3 months.

**Creature types:**
- **Vine-beasts** — Fast, constricting. Dog-to-horse-sized. The most common threat at the edge.
- **Thorn-crawlers** — Ambush predators with paralytic toxins. Burrow into soil and leaf litter.
- **Spore-fungi** — Mobile area-denial organisms. Deploy toxic and narcotic spore clouds.
- **Plant-warriors** — Semi-intelligent humanoid constructs. Appear only during siege-scale assaults.

**Known counters:**
- Fire — Effective but carries severe wildfire risk.
- Salt — Impedes root growth; useful for perimeter defense.
- Edged weapons — Moderate effectiveness against individual creatures.
- Magical wards — Most effective option; suppress growth within radius.

The only permanent solution is disrupting the cathedral-cities' Workings deep within the forest.

## §sensory-palette

A polychrome riot of color — greens, purples, reds, bioluminescent blues that pulse in the deep canopy. The air is thick with pollen and spores, requiring filtered cloth or alchemical masks for prolonged exposure. A constant sound of growth pervades everything — creaking wood, rustling leaves, the wet crack of expanding root systems splitting stone. The smell is sweet-rotten, the cloying scent of organic abundance pushed past all natural limits. Everything moves, even the ground. Soil ripples with root activity. Leaves track movement like eyes.

## §voice-notes

Suldant speak slowly, deliberately, using botanical metaphors. "Your roots are shallow." "Growth requires sacrifice." "The forest remembers what stone forgets." Their voices are deep, resonant, produced by vibrating bark-like vocal structures. Human scouts describe the Verdancy in hushed tones of dread — veterans flinch at the sound of creaking wood. Briefings on Verdancy incursions are delivered in clipped, professional language that barely conceals fear.

## §adventure-hooks

- A Verdancy probe has pushed deeper than usual, establishing a root-node within a mile of NYC's western wall. The node must be destroyed before it anchors permanent growth — but it is guarded by vine-beasts and surrounded by spore-fungi clouds.
- A captured Suldant fragment (a severed root tendril still carrying intelligence) offers to trade information about cathedral-city locations in exchange for being "planted" in fertile soil outside the Verdancy. Its motives are suspect.
- Refugees from a settlement overrun by the Verdancy's latest expansion arrive at NYC carrying seeds they do not know are Verdancy-modified — slow-growing infiltration organisms disguised as normal crops.
- A Black Pact diviner claims to have identified a vulnerability window when one cathedral-city's Workings will be weakened. The expedition would require weeks of travel deep into the Verdancy. The diviner's price is steep and their reliability uncertain.
