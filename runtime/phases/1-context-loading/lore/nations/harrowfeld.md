# Harrowfeld

## §history
Harrowfeld is a breadbasket kingdom — the most productive agricultural region in the Latter Earth, feeding nations that cannot feed themselves. Its prosperity is built on a Legacy irrigation and soil-enrichment system that the original settlers discovered and learned to operate. For two centuries, Harrowfeld has leveraged its food production into political influence, trading grain for security guarantees and diplomatic concessions. It has never needed an army; hunger is a more effective weapon than any sword.

## §geography
Vast, flat plains of extraordinary fertility. The Legacy irrigation system — a network of underground channels, automated pumps, and mineral dispensers — keeps the soil productive regardless of weather. The climate is temperate but the irrigation system moderates it further, preventing drought and frost. Rivers meander through the plains. The landscape is a patchwork of enormous farms, grain silos, and prosperous market towns. There are almost no natural features — the land has been shaped for agriculture since before recorded history.

## §government
An agricultural parliament. Landholders elect representatives based on acreage. The Grain Council — the executive body — is composed of the largest farm-holders. A Steward handles day-to-day governance. Agricultural policy is paramount; all other concerns are secondary. The military is a small professional force supplemented by militia; Harrowfeld relies on its trade partners for defense.

## §culture
Harrowfelders are practical, generous, and quietly proud. They measure wealth in harvests and reputation in the quality of their land. Hospitality is abundant — there is always food. Social gatherings center on communal meals of spectacular abundance. Art is folk art: quilting, pottery, harvest songs. Fashion is plain and durable. Harrowfelders view city-dwellers with amused tolerance and warriors with mild pity.

## §cultural-implications
Agriculture, nature, and trade skill checks are valued. Harrowfeld NPCs are warm and welcoming but shrewd negotiators. They understand the power of food better than anyone. PCs who appreciate good food earn goodwill. Those who waste food earn lasting disapproval. Harrowfelders are slow to anger but devastating when they cut off supply to an offending party.

## §sensory-palette
Harrowfeld is golden — endless wheat fields under blue sky, the warm smell of fresh-turned earth and ripening grain. Harvest season paints the landscape in amber and bronze. The air carries the scent of bread baking in every village. Birdsong is constant. At night, the silos glow with preservation lights, and the hum of the Legacy irrigation system pulses beneath the earth like a heartbeat.

## §voice-notes
Harrowfelders speak with a slow, warm cadence and agricultural metaphors: "That idea needs another season" and "You reap what you sow, but sometimes the soil surprises you." Common greeting: "Have you eaten?" They are generous with advice and food, reserved with criticism. They listen more than they speak and remember everything.

## §adventure-hooks
- The Legacy irrigation system is malfunctioning in one sector, producing crops that grow impossibly fast but are toxic to eat. The malfunction is spreading.
- A foreign power is attempting to seize control of the irrigation system's central hub, hidden beneath the largest farm. They are using purchased land titles to gain legal access.
- Grain shipments to a dependent nation have been poisoned in transit. Harrowfeld is blamed. The real culprit wants to destroy the trade relationship and starve the dependent nation into submission.
- The irrigation system has begun producing something other than water and minerals — a blue fluid with no natural analog. Plants treated with it grow to enormous size and exhibit rudimentary movement.

## §unknown

Deep scans reveal that it extends miles below the surface and connects to systems that have nothing to do with agriculture. The system's true purpose may not be growing food — feeding the surface population may be a side effect of its actual function, which remains [UNKNOWN].
