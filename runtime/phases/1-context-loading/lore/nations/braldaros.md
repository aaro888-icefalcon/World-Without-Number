# Braldaros

## §history
Braldaros was forged in conquest. Three centuries ago, the warlord Brald the Ironhand united the feuding clans of the northern highlands through a combination of military genius and ruthless pragmatism. His descendants rule still, though the current king is a far cry from his ancestor — a cautious diplomat who prefers trade to war. The nation's martial traditions remain strong, however, and every citizen serves a mandatory term in the border garrisons.

## §geography
Rugged highlands cut by deep valleys and fast rivers. The Ironteeth mountains form the western border. Winters are long and harsh; summers brief but intensely green. Dense pine forests cover the lower slopes, giving way to bare rock and snow above. The soil is poor for farming but rich in iron, copper, and tin.

## §government
A feudal monarchy. The king rules from the fortress-city of Braldheim, supported by a council of clan chiefs who hold hereditary lands. Succession follows the eldest child regardless of gender. Disputes between clans are settled by formal combat or royal arbitration.

## §culture
Braldans are blunt, physical, and fiercely loyal to clan and kin. Hospitality is sacred — turning away a guest brings lasting shame. Oral storytelling is the primary art form, with skalds holding honored positions in every household. Metalwork is the most respected craft. Meals are communal, heavy, and accompanied by strong ale.

## §cultural-implications
Physical skill checks (climbing, endurance, combat) are culturally respected. Braldans distrust magic-users and overly clever arguments. Social checks work best when direct and honest — flattery is seen as weakness.

## §sensory-palette
The air of Braldaros is sharp with pine resin and wood smoke. The clang of hammers on anvils echoes through every settlement. In winter, the world narrows to grey sky, white snow, and the warm orange glow of hearthfires glimpsed through frosted windows.

## §voice-notes
Braldans speak in short, declarative sentences. They use concrete metaphors drawn from smithing and weather. Common expressions: "Strong as forge-steel" and "That wind won't blow twice." They speak loudly and consider soft speech suspicious.

## §adventure-hooks
- A mine in the western mountains has broken into a Legacy complex. Miners report strange lights and missing workers.
- A clan chief's heir has vanished during a solo hunt in the deep forest — a rite of passage that should have taken three days. It has been three weeks.
- A foreign merchant is selling weapons of unusual quality at suspiciously low prices. The weapons bear markings no smith can identify.
- The king's health is failing and two rival clan chiefs are positioning for influence over the succession.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The deepest mines of the Ironteeth occasionally breach chambers filled with warm, breathable air and strange vegetation. The miners seal these breaches and do not speak of them. What ecosystem exists beneath the mountains is entirely uncharted.
