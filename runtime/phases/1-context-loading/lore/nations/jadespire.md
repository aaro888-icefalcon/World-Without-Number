# Jadespire

## §history
Jadespire takes its name from the enormous jade-green crystal spire that dominates its capital — a Legacy structure of unknown origin that predates all recorded history. The nation formed around this landmark as pilgrims, scholars, and opportunists gathered to study it. Over centuries, the settlement grew into a prosperous city-state, then expanded through diplomacy and marriage alliances to encompass the surrounding fertile valleys. Jadespire has never fought an offensive war; its spire's defensive properties make it nearly impervious to siege.

## §geography
A broad river valley surrounded by gentle hills, centered on the capital city and its iconic spire. The climate is warm and temperate with reliable rainfall. The soil is exceptionally fertile — some scholars attribute this to emanations from the spire. Orchards, vineyards, and flower gardens are everywhere. The landscape feels curated rather than wild.

## §government
An elective monarchy. The sovereign is chosen from among candidates who demonstrate attunement with the jade spire — a process that involves entering the structure and emerging alive. The sovereign rules with the aid of a parliament of landed gentry and guild representatives. Governance is consensual and generally competent.

## §culture
Jadespiri culture is aesthetic, contemplative, and deeply influenced by the spire's presence. Art — particularly glass-blowing, gem-cutting, and crystalline sculpture — dominates cultural life. Philosophy and meditation are widely practiced. The people are welcoming to outsiders and genuinely curious about foreign customs. Festivals are frequent and joyous.

## §cultural-implications
Artistic and scholarly skill checks are well-received. PCs with knowledge of Legacy artifacts or crystalline structures gain special access and respect. Violence is culturally abhorrent — aggressive PCs face social penalties. Jadespiri NPCs are open-minded but will not tolerate cruelty.

## §sensory-palette
Jadespire is a city of light. The jade spire catches sunlight and refracts it across the valley in shifting green and gold patterns. The air smells of blossoming trees and warm stone. Wind chimes of crystal and glass sing from every eave. At night, the spire glows faintly from within, casting the city in emerald twilight.

## §voice-notes
Jadespiri speak in flowing, unhurried sentences with a musical quality. They ask questions more than they make statements. Common expressions: "The spire shows many faces" and "Let us see what light reveals." They touch their foreheads when greeting strangers — a gesture of open-mindedness.

## §adventure-hooks
- The jade spire has begun emitting a tone audible only to certain individuals. Those who hear it are drawn to it irresistibly and cannot explain why.
- A section of the spire's interior that was previously solid wall has become transparent, revealing chambers and passages within. No one can find an entrance.
- A foreign scholar claims to have decoded inscriptions on the spire's base. Her translation suggests the spire is a beacon — but a beacon for what?
- The most recent sovereign selection ended without a candidate. Every aspirant who entered the spire emerged catatonic. The throne sits empty.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The jade spire extends as far below ground as it rises above. Its deepest reaches have never been explored. The structure does not appear on any map from prior ages, leading some scholars to theorize it was placed in its current location, fully formed, at a specific moment in history. By whom, and why, is the nation's founding mystery.
