# Kaldria

## §history
Kaldria is a young nation born from revolution. A century ago, the merchant class of the coastal cities overthrew their feudal lords in a bloody uprising. The resulting republic was chaotic at first but has stabilized into a functioning democracy — the only true democracy in the known world. The revolution's ideals of liberty, equality, and rational governance are enshrined in a written constitution and fiercely defended.

## §geography
A temperate coastal region with rolling farmland, busy ports, and prosperous market towns. The coastline is dotted with natural harbors along the Sunward Sea. Inland, the terrain rises to forested hills. Rivers are short but navigable, supporting trade between coast and interior. The climate is mild with cool winters and warm summers.

## §government
A constitutional republic. Citizens elect representatives to a National Assembly, which legislates and appoints an executive council. The judiciary is independent. Suffrage is extended to all property-owning adults regardless of gender or birth. Political parties compete vigorously, and elections are passionate, occasionally violent affairs.

## §culture
Kaldrians are argumentative, idealistic, and intensely political. Every tavern is a debating chamber. Newspapers proliferate. The arts favor satire, political theater, and protest songs. Individual rights are paramount. Kaldrians distrust authority on principle and question everything. Food and wine are excellent — revolution did not diminish the culinary tradition.

## §cultural-implications
Persuasion and rhetoric skill checks are highly effective. Kaldrian NPCs respond well to reasoned arguments and poorly to appeals to authority. Attempting to pull rank or intimidate provokes defiance. Knowledge of political philosophy earns respect.

## §sensory-palette
Kaldria buzzes with conversation. Cafes overflow onto cobblestone streets where pamphleteers hawk broadsheets and street orators declaim from corners. The air smells of fresh bread, printer's ink, and sea salt. Buildings are modest but well-maintained, painted in cheerful colors. Somewhere, always, someone is arguing.

## §voice-notes
Kaldrians speak with passionate intensity, using rhetorical questions and emphatic gestures. Common expressions: "Liberty is not given, it is taken" and "The Assembly will hear of this!" They debate for sport and take no offense at disagreement — only at indifference.

## §adventure-hooks
- A charismatic demagogue is exploiting economic discontent to push for authoritarian reforms. The Assembly is divided on how to respond.
- The national archives contain a pre-revolution document suggesting the old feudal lords had a legitimate reason for some of their most repressive policies — one involving a sealed Legacy threat beneath the capital.
- A foreign spy ring is manipulating elections through bribery and blackmail. The evidence points to multiple nations as possible sponsors.
- A group of radical republicans wants to export the revolution to neighboring monarchies. Their first target is a powerful and dangerous kingdom.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The revolution's success was unexpectedly swift. Historians note that the feudal lords capitulated almost simultaneously, as if responding to a signal. Whether they were compelled by an outside force, or whether they chose to fall for reasons not yet understood, remains a troubling gap in Kaldria's founding narrative.
