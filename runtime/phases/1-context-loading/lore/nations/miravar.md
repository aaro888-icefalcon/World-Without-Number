# Miravar

## §geography
Miravar lies within an enormous river delta where the Threefold River splits into dozens of channels before reaching the Pale Sea. The terrain is flat, lush, and perpetually waterlogged, a labyrinth of islands, sandbars, and mangrove forests that shift with the seasons. The climate is tropical, hot and humid year-round, with a monsoon season that turns much of the delta into open water.

## §government
A merchant republic governed by the Grand Confluence, a legislative body whose seats are purchased through annual auction. Only those who hold Confluence seats may vote on law and policy. In practice, this means the wealthiest trading houses control the government, though a tradition of philanthropic obligation tempers the worst excesses of plutocracy.

## §culture
Miravari are traders and negotiators by nature. Commerce is not merely an occupation but a philosophy; they believe that fair exchange is the highest form of human interaction. Contracts are sacred and enforced by an independent judiciary. Art and architecture celebrate abundance and interconnection. The floating markets of the delta are legendary, and festivals involve elaborate gift exchanges between households.

## §economy
Miravar is the Latter Earth's preeminent trading hub, its delta location providing access to both river and ocean trade routes. Warehouses line every stable island, and the nation's currency, the silver reed, is accepted across multiple continents. Miravar produces little raw material itself but adds enormous value through processing, banking, insurance, and brokerage services.

## §military
Miravar maintains a modest professional navy of shallow-draft riverine warships and a small marine force. Its true defense is economic: Miravar has made itself so essential to international trade that attacking it would harm the aggressor's own commerce. The nation also retains a corps of mercenary companies on permanent contract, drawn from a dozen nations.

## §religion
Miravari worship is eclectic, reflecting the delta's role as a cultural crossroads. Temples to dozens of deities line the major waterways. The most distinctive local tradition venerates the River Itself as a living entity, with offerings cast into the current at every bridge and dock. Priests of the River read the water's color, speed, and taste to divine its mood.

## §history
Miravar was founded two centuries ago when three rival merchant families, exhausted by trade wars, agreed to pool their resources and establish a shared market. The Grand Confluence was created as the governing mechanism for this arrangement. The nation has grown wealthy beyond measure but has twice nearly collapsed due to financial crises when major trading partners defaulted on debts.
