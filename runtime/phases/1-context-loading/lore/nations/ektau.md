# Ektau

## §history
Ektau is a confederation of nomadic tribes that roam the great steppe east of the Spine of the World. Unlike most nations, Ektau has no fixed capital and no permanent borders. Its unity stems from a shared legal code — the Riding Law — established by the legendary chieftain Ek the Thunderer two centuries ago. The tribes gather annually at the Great Moot to settle disputes, arrange marriages, and elect a war-chief if external threats demand unified action.

## §geography
Endless grasslands stretching from the foothills of the Spine to the edges of the eastern deserts. The steppe is flat, windswept, and seemingly featureless to outsiders, though the Ektau read its subtle variations like a book. Winters bring deadly blizzards; summers are hot and dry. Scattered oases and seasonal rivers provide water. Legacy ruins dot the landscape, half-buried in grass and earth.

## §government
A tribal confederation governed by the Riding Law. Each tribe is led by a khan elected by the warriors. The Great Moot settles inter-tribal disputes and can elect a Grand Khan in times of crisis. No permanent bureaucracy exists. Justice is swift and personal.

## §culture
Ektau culture centers on horsemanship, herding, and the freedom of the open steppe. Horses are sacred — harming one is punishable by death. Oral poetry and throat-singing are the primary art forms. Hospitality to travelers is absolute, as is the obligation to avenge insults to honor. Material possessions are few; wealth is measured in horses and reputation.

## §cultural-implications
Riding and animal handling checks are respected. Showing fear or physical weakness invites contempt. Ektau NPCs value courage, honesty, and generosity. Attempts at deception are poorly received — the steppe teaches people to read faces.

## §sensory-palette
The steppe is an ocean of grass under an enormous sky. The wind never stops — it carries the smell of horse, dried dung fires, and wild thyme. At night, the stars blaze with ferocious clarity, and the distant howl of steppe wolves threads through the silence.

## §voice-notes
Ektau speak with a rhythmic, rising cadence, as though every statement might become a song. They use nature metaphors: "Swift as the hawk's shadow" and "The grass remembers what the wind forgets." They laugh easily and loudly, and silence from an Ektau means danger.

## §adventure-hooks
- A Legacy ruin has risen from the earth after a recent earthquake, fully intact. Multiple tribes are converging on it, and conflict seems inevitable.
- A tribe's sacred horse herd has been struck by a wasting disease that no healer can cure. The symptoms match descriptions in ancient texts of sorcerous bioweapons.
- A foreign power is arming a renegade khan with advanced weapons. The Great Moot needs proof before it can act.
- The annual Great Moot has been disrupted by the appearance of a figure claiming to be Ek the Thunderer, returned from death.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The steppe contains areas the Ektau refuse to enter, marked by ancient stone cairns. They say the ground there "remembers pain." Outsiders who have entered these zones report temporal disorientation and vivid hallucinations of battles fought in prior ages.
