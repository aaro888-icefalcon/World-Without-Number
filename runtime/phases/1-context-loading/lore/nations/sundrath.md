# Sundrath

## §geography
Sundrath encompasses a vast savanna and grassland region south of the great desert belt, where golden plains stretch unbroken to every horizon. Scattered acacia-like trees and rocky kopjes punctuate the landscape. The climate follows a strict wet-dry cycle, with months of torrential rain followed by months of sun-baked dryness. Great herds of megafauna migrate across the plains in seasonal patterns.

## §government
A confederation of nomadic clans united under the Sunlord, a war-chief chosen by trial of arms and confirmed by the approval of the clan shamans. The Sunlord's authority is absolute in matters of war and inter-clan disputes but does not extend to internal clan governance. Each clan is led by its own elder council. Power transitions are frequent and occasionally violent.

## §culture
Sundrathi are pastoralists and hunters who follow the great herds across the plains. Wealth is measured in cattle and horses. Oral history, dance, and drumming form the core of cultural expression. Coming-of-age rituals require young adults to survive alone on the savanna for a full lunar cycle. Sundrathi value courage, generosity, and eloquent speech, and their moots feature hours of formal oratory.

## §economy
Cattle, horses, and hides form the primary exports, along with ivory, rare animal products, and medicinal plants found only on the savanna. Sundrathi horse-breeders produce the finest war-mounts on the continent. Trade is conducted at seasonal gathering sites where clans converge and foreign merchants are permitted to set up temporary markets.

## §military
Sundrathi cavalry is the finest on the continent — fast, disciplined, and devastatingly effective on open ground. Riders fight with compound bows and long lances, employing tactics of encirclement and feigned retreat perfected over centuries. Every adult is a warrior; the distinction between civilian and soldier does not exist in Sundrathi culture.

## §religion
The Sundrathi venerate the Sky Father and Earth Mother as a paired duality governing all existence. Shamans serve as intermediaries, entering trance states through rhythmic drumming and sacred plant preparations. Ancestor spirits are believed to ride alongside the living during battle. Sacred sites are marked by standing stones arranged in precise astronomical alignments that predate Sundrathi settlement.

## §history
Sundrathi oral tradition holds that their ancestors emerged from a great underground city when the world above became habitable again after an ancient catastrophe. The location of this origin-city has been lost, and its rediscovery is a recurring theme in Sundrathi legend. Two centuries ago, the clans united under a legendary Sunlord to repel an invasion from the north, a victory still celebrated as the defining moment of national identity.

## §current-affairs
The great herds have altered their migration pattern for the first time in recorded memory, converging on a region of the savanna that clans traditionally avoid. The shamans speak of the animals being called. Scouts sent to investigate report that the standing stones in the forbidden region have begun to hum at frequencies felt in the bones rather than heard.
