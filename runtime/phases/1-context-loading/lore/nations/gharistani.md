# Gharistani

## §history
Gharistani is a proud warrior culture that controls the high mountain passes connecting the eastern and western lands. For centuries, they have extracted tolls from trade caravans and fought off every empire that attempted to absorb them. The nation was founded by twelve warrior clans who swore a blood oath of mutual defense atop the peak of Mount Gharis. That oath — the Pact of Twelve Blades — remains the foundation of Gharistani law and identity.

## §geography
High mountain valleys and steep passes in the central portion of the Spine of the World. The terrain is dramatic — soaring peaks, deep gorges, and alpine meadows. Rivers run fast and cold. The climate is harsh, with heavy snowfall in winter and brief, cool summers. Terraced agriculture clings to valley slopes. The mountain passes are the only viable overland trade routes between east and west.

## §government
A council of clan elders, one from each of the twelve founding clans. Decisions require unanimous consent, which makes governance slow but ensures no clan is overridden. A war-leader is elected during conflicts and holds absolute authority until the threat passes, after which they must relinquish power or face collective opposition.

## §culture
Gharistani prize independence, martial skill, and personal honor above all else. Feuds between clans are common but governed by strict rules of engagement. Poetry and music — particularly the mountain flute — are cherished. Hospitality to travelers is a sacred duty, but guests must respect local customs absolutely. Meals are simple: bread, cheese, dried meat, and bitter mountain tea.

## §cultural-implications
Martial prowess earns respect. PCs who demonstrate physical courage or weapon skill gain social standing. Displays of wealth or arrogance provoke hostility. Knowledge of Gharistani customs is essential — accidental insults to honor can escalate rapidly.

## §sensory-palette
Thin, cold air that burns the lungs. The smell of pine smoke and yak wool. Mist clings to the valleys at dawn, revealing stone villages perched impossibly on cliff faces. The sound of rushing water is constant, underlaid by the distant clatter of goat bells and the haunting cry of mountain flutes at dusk.

## §voice-notes
Gharistani speak with a clipped, emphatic rhythm. They state facts; they do not speculate. Common expressions: "The mountain does not bend" and "Blood remembers." They look people in the eye when speaking and consider averted gaze a sign of dishonesty.

## §adventure-hooks
- A previously unknown pass has opened due to a landslide, revealing a Legacy road of perfectly preserved stone leading into an unmapped valley.
- One of the twelve clans has been massacred in a single night. The survivors speak of attackers who moved like shadows and left no tracks.
- A foreign empire is attempting to tunnel through the mountains to bypass the Gharistani passes. The tunneling has disturbed something underground.
- An ancient weapon-shrine atop Mount Gharis has begun to glow. The clan elders cannot agree on whether to investigate or seal it permanently.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The Pact of Twelve Blades is said to have been sworn in the presence of a thirteenth party — a being or entity that guaranteed the oath. The identity of this guarantor has been erased from all records. The clan elders know more than they reveal.
