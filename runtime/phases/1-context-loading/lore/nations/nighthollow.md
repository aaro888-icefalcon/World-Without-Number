# Nighthollow

## §history
Nighthollow exists in perpetual darkness. A Legacy device buried deep beneath the valley activated two centuries ago, projecting a field that blocks all sunlight within a fifty-mile radius. The farming communities in the valley faced a choice: flee or adapt. Many fled. Those who remained learned to live in the dark, developing new agricultural techniques (bioluminescent crops, fungal farming), new senses, and a new culture shaped entirely by the absence of light. Nighthollow is now a thriving, if deeply strange, community that no outsider is quite prepared for.

## §geography
A broad valley surrounded by hills, capped by an impenetrable dome of darkness. From outside, the boundary is visible as a wall of absolute black. Inside, there is no sunlight, no moonlight, no starlight. Bioluminescent organisms — fungi, insects, modified plants — provide dim illumination. Rivers still flow, rain still falls (the dome blocks light, not weather), and the temperature is cool but stable. Underground Legacy chambers beneath the valley house the device that maintains the darkness.

## §government
A council of Listeners — individuals who have developed extraordinary non-visual senses and serve as the community's navigators, judges, and leaders. The Chief Listener governs by consensus. Disputes are settled through a formal process of testimony delivered in total silence, where only the truth of a speaker's voice — its stress, its breathing, its micro-hesitations — determines the verdict.

## §culture
Nighthollow culture has been entirely reshaped by darkness. Touch, sound, and smell dominate social interaction. Music is the supreme art — Nighthollow produces the most accomplished musicians in the Latter Earth, their hearing refined by lives without visual distraction. Physical contact is casual and constant — touching is how Nighthollowers navigate relationships. Food is evaluated by texture, smell, and temperature. Beauty is defined by sound, not sight.

## §cultural-implications
Perception (non-visual), stealth, and performance skill checks are valued. Nighthollow NPCs navigate entirely by sound and touch — visual disguises are useless, but voice and scent manipulation are powerful. PCs who are blind or comfortable in darkness have a significant advantage. Those who depend on sight are at a serious disadvantage.

## §sensory-palette
Nighthollow is sound and touch. The drip of water on stone, the whisper of fungal spores drifting, the soft shuffle of bare feet on smooth paths. The air smells of mycelium, damp earth, and the faint sweetness of bioluminescent blooms. Dim blue-green light from cultivated fungi outlines but does not illuminate. Everything is felt: the texture of walls, the warmth of another person, the vibration of distant machinery.

## §voice-notes
Nighthollowers speak with extraordinary vocal control — pitch, tone, and volume convey meaning as much as words. They rarely raise their voices; a whisper carries in the silence. Common expressions: "I hear you" (acknowledgment, not just auditory), "The dark provides" (equivalent to a blessing), and "Walk softly." They identify individuals by voice and footstep, not by name.

## §adventure-hooks
- The darkness dome is flickering. Brief flashes of sunlight are penetrating — blinding and terrifying to residents who have never experienced it. The Legacy device beneath the valley is failing. The Listeners are divided: some want it repaired, others want it destroyed.
- An outsider has entered Nighthollow and disappeared. Their companions want them found. Searching in total darkness for someone who does not want to be found presents unique challenges.
- A Listener has begun hearing a voice from the Legacy device — not mechanical instructions, but conversation. The voice claims to be the device's creator and says the darkness was not an accident but a protection against something that hunts by sight.
- Nighthollow's bioluminescent crops are dying, replaced by a new organism that provides brighter light but induces euphoria and dependency in those exposed to it. The source organism was not naturally evolved.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The Legacy device that creates the darkness dome may not be malfunctioning at all. The darkness may be exactly what it was designed to produce. Archaeological evidence suggests the device was activated deliberately, at a specific moment, in response to a specific threat. What that threat was — and whether it still exists outside the dome — is the question that defines Nighthollow's relationship with the outside world.
