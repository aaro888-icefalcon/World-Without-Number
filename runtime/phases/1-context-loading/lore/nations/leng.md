# Leng

## §history
Leng is ancient beyond reckoning — its people claim unbroken habitation since the Third Age, and archaeological evidence partially supports this. The high plateau has served as a refuge during every cataclysm, its altitude and isolation providing natural protection. Leng has never been conquered, though it has never sought to conquer others. Its monasteries preserve fragments of knowledge from every age, making it a repository of lore found nowhere else.

## §geography
A vast, windswept plateau at extreme altitude, ringed by impassable mountains on three sides. The air is thin and cold. The landscape is stark — rocky plains, glacial lakes of startling blue, and sparse vegetation. Summers are cool; winters are lethal to the unprepared. The plateau holds scattered oases of geothermal warmth where hot springs support small settlements.

## §government
A monastic theocracy. The Abbots of the great monasteries govern by consensus, guided by contemplative traditions stretching back millennia. The High Abbot is chosen through a process of divination and meditation that takes years. Secular affairs are handled by village councils; the monasteries concern themselves with spiritual and scholarly matters.

## §culture
Lengese culture is contemplative, austere, and deeply spiritual. Silence is valued. Physical endurance is admired — survival on the plateau demands it. Art takes the form of sand painting, mantra chanting, and intricate textile weaving. Tea made from high-altitude herbs is the universal social lubricant. Material ambition is discouraged; inner development is the measure of a person.

## §cultural-implications
Patience and spiritual awareness earn respect. Aggressive or materialistic PCs face social friction. Lengese NPCs are perceptive — deception checks are harder, as they study people intently. Knowledge of mystical or philosophical traditions opens doors.

## §sensory-palette
Leng is a world of sky and stone. The air is so clear that mountains a hundred miles distant appear arm's reach away. Prayer flags snap in the constant wind. The smell of juniper incense and yak butter tea drifts from monastery windows. At night, the stars are so dense they cast shadows.

## §voice-notes
Lengese speak slowly and softly, choosing words with care. They use paradox and metaphor: "The empty cup is already full" and "Walk slowly to arrive quickly." Long pauses mid-conversation are normal — they are thinking, not ignoring you. They rarely ask personal questions of strangers.

## §adventure-hooks
- A monastery has been sealed from within. No communication has emerged in months. The other abbots are concerned but hesitant to force entry — the monastery guards something ancient.
- A scholar from Athkarith has arrived seeking a specific text preserved in Leng. The abbots have refused access. The scholar is growing desperate and may take drastic action.
- Geothermal activity has increased dramatically. Hot springs are boiling over, and new vents are opening. The monasteries' ancient records describe this as a precursor to something they call the "Deep Waking."
- A young monk has manifested abilities that do not correspond to any known magical tradition. The abbots believe she is channeling something from beneath the plateau.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The monasteries guard their deepest archives with absolute secrecy. Certain texts are read only by the High Abbot, and some chambers have not been opened in living memory. What ultimate truth the monasteries protect — or what threat they contain — is Leng's most profound mystery.
