# Qaedra

## §history
Qaedra is a desert queendom ruled by an unbroken matrilineal dynasty for over five centuries — the longest continuous rule in the Fifth Age. The first queen, Qaed the Unifier, rallied the scattered desert tribes against a Sorcerer-King's remnant that terrorized the region. After its destruction, she established a kingdom built on three pillars: water management, astronomical knowledge, and martial discipline. Her descendants have maintained all three with remarkable consistency.

## §geography
A vast desert punctuated by deep oases, sandstone mesas, and dry riverbeds that flow only during the brief rainy season. The western edge borders a mountain range that traps moisture, creating a narrow fertile strip. Underground aquifers, mapped and managed by royal engineers, sustain the population. Nights are bitterly cold; days are scorching. Sandstorms can last for days.

## §government
A matrilineal absolute monarchy. The queen rules with the counsel of the Water Court — a body of engineers, astronomers, and military commanders. Succession passes to the queen's eldest daughter or nearest female relative. Male members of the royal family serve as military commanders and diplomats. Provincial governance is delegated to appointed matriarchs.

## §culture
Qaedran culture is disciplined, proud, and deeply connected to the stars. Astronomy is the supreme science — navigation, agriculture, and religious practice all depend on stellar observation. Hospitality to travelers is law, but so is the expectation that guests contribute to the community. Poetry and stargazing are the primary leisure activities. Water waste is a criminal offense.

## §cultural-implications
Navigation, survival, and astronomy skill checks are valued. Qaedran NPCs respect discipline and self-sufficiency. Wastefulness of any kind provokes disgust. Female PCs may find certain doors open more readily, though male PCs are not disrespected — merely expected to defer on matters of governance.

## §sensory-palette
Qaedra is a land of stark beauty — rust-red sandstone against deep blue sky, the glitter of stars so numerous they illuminate the desert at night. The air is dry and carries the scent of sand, myrrh, and roasting coffee. Oasis settlements are cool and green, fragrant with date palms and jasmine. The silence of the deep desert is absolute and unsettling.

## §voice-notes
Qaedrans speak with deliberate formality and a melodic rhythm. They use astronomical metaphors: "Your path is well-starred" and "That plan has no horizon." Common greeting: "May your water never fail." They speak truthfully and consider lies a form of spiritual pollution.

## §adventure-hooks
- The royal astronomers have detected an anomaly in the night sky — a star that should not exist, growing brighter each night. Ancient texts describe a similar phenomenon before the Shattering.
- An aquifer that has sustained a major oasis for centuries has suddenly gone dry. Underground exploration reveals the water has been diverted — deliberately — into a Legacy structure below the desert.
- A rival claimant to the throne has appeared, bearing a Legacy artifact that allegedly proves an older matrilineal line. The artifact's authenticity is [UNKNOWN].
- Desert nomads report finding a glass-smooth crater in the deep desert, still warm to the touch. Something fell from the sky — or was launched from below.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — Qaed the Unifier did not simply destroy the Sorcerer-King's remnant. Royal archives, sealed to all but the ruling queen, suggest she made a bargain with it. The nature of that bargain, and whether it still binds the dynasty, is the kingdom's most dangerous secret.
