# Dulimbai

## §history
Dulimbai is the largest and most bureaucratically complex nation in the Latter Earth. Founded four centuries ago when a military governor unified the fertile central plains through a combination of conquest and administrative reform, it has since grown into a sprawling empire governed by an elaborate civil service. The current Celestial Mandate — the hereditary emperor — is largely ceremonial. Real power rests with the ministries.

## §geography
Vast alluvial plains bisected by the Greywater river and its tributaries. The land is flat, fertile, and extensively cultivated. Rice paddies, wheat fields, and orchards stretch to every horizon. The climate is temperate to subtropical, with monsoon rains in summer. The eastern coast faces the Sunward Sea.

## §government
A bureaucratic empire. The Celestial Mandate reigns; the Six Ministries rule. Entry to the civil service requires passing a series of rigorous examinations testing knowledge of law, history, mathematics, and ethics. Corruption exists but is punished harshly when discovered. Provincial governors rotate every five years to prevent entrenchment.

## §culture
Dulimbi culture values order, propriety, and collective welfare above individual ambition. Family obligations are paramount. The arts — calligraphy, landscape painting, formal poetry — are refined to extraordinary levels. Tea ceremonies accompany all significant social interactions. Social hierarchy is complex and meticulously observed.

## §cultural-implications
Etiquette and protocol matter enormously. PCs who understand Dulimbi customs gain significant social advantages. Bureaucratic navigation requires patience and skill. Direct confrontation is avoided — problems are solved through intermediaries and carefully worded suggestions.

## §sensory-palette
Dulimbai is a land of green — terraced rice paddies shimmering under grey monsoon skies, willows trailing in slow canals, moss-covered stone bridges. The air smells of damp earth, cooking rice, and incense from roadside shrines. Cities hum with the murmur of commerce and the click of abacus beads.

## §voice-notes
Dulimbi speak formally, with elaborate courtesy formulas. They use indirect language: "It would perhaps be unfortunate if..." rather than "Don't do that." Common expressions: "The river finds its way" and "Patience fills the granary." They speak softly and consider loudness a sign of poor breeding.

## §adventure-hooks
- A provincial governor has stopped responding to ministerial dispatches. The last messenger sent to investigate has not returned.
- An ancient irrigation canal has collapsed, revealing a sealed Legacy vault beneath. The local magistrate wants it explored before the ministry sends its own agents.
- A series of impossible thefts has struck the imperial archives. Documents are vanishing from sealed rooms with no sign of entry.
- Peasant uprisings in the western provinces are being led by a figure who claims to channel a Sorcerer-King's authority.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The Celestial Mandate's palace contains a sealed inner sanctum that predates the nation by millennia. Each emperor enters it once upon coronation and emerges changed. What they see inside is never spoken of, but every emperor thereafter rules with an unsettling calm.
