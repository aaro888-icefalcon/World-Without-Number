# Hecton

## §history
Hecton is built atop the largest concentration of functioning Legacy infrastructure in the known world. The city-state's founders discovered a Third Age industrial complex beneath the coastal cliffs and learned to operate fragments of its machinery. Over two centuries, Hecton has leveraged this technological advantage into regional dominance, producing goods of a quality no other nation can match. The ruling Artificer's Guild guards its monopoly with paranoid intensity.

## §geography
A densely populated city-state occupying a rocky peninsula jutting into the Sunward Sea. The surface city is compact and vertical, built into the cliff face. Below, the Legacy complex extends deep into the earth — its full extent is unmapped. The surrounding countryside is limited to a narrow strip of arable land. The climate is mild and maritime.

## §government
A technocratic oligarchy. The Artificer's Guild governs through a council of Master Artificers, each responsible for a different sector of the Legacy complex. Non-Guild citizens have limited political rights. Guild membership requires demonstrated aptitude with Legacy technology and a decade of apprenticeship.

## §culture
Hectoni culture is pragmatic, secretive, and intensely competitive. Innovation is the highest virtue, but sharing knowledge outside the Guild is treason. Social status correlates directly with technical skill. Art is functional — beautiful machines, elegant mechanisms. Food is imported and unremarkable. Entertainment centers on mechanical exhibitions and puzzle-solving competitions.

## §cultural-implications
Technical and engineering skill checks are valued. PCs with knowledge of Legacy technology receive preferential treatment. The Guild is suspicious of outsiders and mages — magic and technology interact unpredictably. Information is currency, and secrets are weapons.

## §sensory-palette
Hecton vibrates. The Legacy machinery beneath the city produces a constant subsonic hum felt in the bones. The air tastes of metal and ozone. Streets are lit by Legacy light-strips that never dim. The clank and hiss of mechanisms emerges from every workshop, and thin trails of steam rise from vents in the cobblestones.

## §voice-notes
Hectoni speak in precise, technical language even in casual conversation. They quantify everything: "A seven-out-of-ten situation" or "That plan has maybe a thirty percent yield." Common expressions: "If it works, it's true" and "Don't open a mechanism you can't close." They speak quickly and impatiently.

## §adventure-hooks
- A section of the Legacy complex has activated autonomously, sealing itself off. Workers trapped inside are sending increasingly desperate signals.
- A rival nation has somehow acquired Hectoni-quality goods without buying them. The Guild suspects espionage and wants the leak found.
- The Legacy complex's power output has been declining for months. Deep exploration teams sent to investigate the core have not returned.
- A young apprentice has discovered she can interface with Legacy systems mentally — an ability that should not exist. The Guild is divided between those who want to study her and those who want her eliminated.

## §unknown
[UNKNOWN] — The deepest mapped level of the Legacy complex contains a sealed chamber with a door that opens only once per year, at midwinter midnight, for exactly sixty seconds. No one has ever entered and returned. The Guild posts guards but does not attempt entry. What lies beyond is the city's most closely guarded question.
