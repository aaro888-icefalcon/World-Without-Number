# Magic Manifestation Among NYC's Population

## Core Mechanism

The Legacy's interaction with the transport event randomly activated latent magical potential in a fraction of NYC's population. Most develop no class-level abilities. Player characters and notable NPCs experienced a Legacy surge during translocation that granted capabilities normally requiring a lifetime of attunement. Activation is random from character's perspective — a janitor may become a Mage, a cop may become a Mageslayer.

All WWN, WWN Deluxe, and Atlas classes available at full effect from Day 1. Narrative explanation: Legacy surge granted instantaneous attunement equivalent to native Latter Earth magic-users' lifetime of training. Character doesn't understand abilities the way a trained mage would — learns through use, experimentation, and mentorship.

## Foci Manifestation

One starting Focus at character creation, emerging from pre-transport background. Legacy amplifies existing competencies into supernatural expression.

Examples by background:

- Former cop → Danger Sense (supernatural threat awareness, "gut feeling" becomes near-precognition)
- Former EMT → Healer's Touch (enhanced medical treatment, feel injuries through contact)
- Former ironworker → Impervious Defense (enhanced physical resistance, toughened skin)
- Former subway engineer → Deep Sense (perceive gallery structure through stone, sense voids/passages/water/carvers)
- Former analyst → Pattern Reading (perceive Emperor's carved patterns as information, read gallery walls as navigational/historical data)
- Former martial artist → Ki Strike (supernatural striking power, eventually channeling Legacy energy)
- Former firefighter → Flame Dominion (control fire, direct/suppress/generate, critical on Verdancy front)

Additional Foci develop through experience as in standard WWN.

## Gallery Attunement

Prolonged deep-gallery exposure accelerates magical development. Tracked as attunement strain — Legacy-induced changes to body and mind.

- **Mild strain (early gallery exposure):** Enhanced magical perception. See Workings as faint distortions. Minor sensory changes.
- **Moderate strain (sustained deep work):** Can interact with simple Workings. Physical changes begin: faint luminescence, skin texture changes, altered sleep. Personality shifts: heightened intensity, gallery fascination.
- **Severe strain (prolonged Level 4+):** Full magical development for spellcasting classes. Significant physical transformation: visible luminescence, mineral/crystalline skin qualities, temperature fluctuation. Difficulty relating to non-attuned. Compulsive attraction to depths. Dreams of Emperor's echoes.
- **Extreme strain (Level 5+ sustained):** Maximum capability. Profound transformation — partial adaptation makes surface life uncomfortable. Character becoming something other than baseline human. Social consequences mirror Gadavin Coast's Touched spectrum — feared and revered.

Attunement strain does not reset on character death. Heavily attuned character represents significant investment; loss is devastating.

## External Magical Training

NYC characters can seek mentorship from native practitioners. Each source has political strings:

- **Black Pact sorcerers:** Powerful instruction with demonic-patron theological framework. Price: contractual service, infernal entanglement.
- **Still City undead wizards:** Necromantic knowledge accumulated over millennia. Price: moral compromise, undead-politics entanglement.
- **Amundi priest-sorcerers:** Divine magic tied to Amundi gods (mostly dead, power lingers). Price: religious observance, political obligation.
- **Courontine monks (Bleeding God):** Healing and endurance magic through suffering-theology. Price: devotion and service.

Player's choice of mentor shapes abilities and factional alignment simultaneously.
