# Geography of the Latter Earth

## §overview

The geography of the Latter Earth bears the scars of ages of civilization, warfare, and cataclysm. Mountain ranges were raised by Titan wars. Deserts mark where sorcerous weapons burned the land barren. Rivers follow channels carved by ancient engineering. No landscape is truly natural — everything has been shaped, broken, and reshaped by the forces of prior ages.

## §major-seas-and-oceans

- **The Sunward Sea**: The great warm ocean separating the western and eastern continents. Rich in trade routes and piracy. Its depths hold drowned cities from at least three prior ages.
- **The Nightwater**: The cold northern ocean. Largely unnavigable due to storms, ice, and [UNKNOWN] hazards. Coastal peoples report lights beneath the surface.
- **The Jade Gulf**: A warm, shallow sea between the southern coasts. Known for its green-tinted waters (caused by Legacy mineral deposits) and abundant fishing.
- **The Shattered Strait**: A narrow, island-choked waterway connecting major seas. The islands are remnants of a landmass destroyed during the Wars of Unmaking.
- **The Abyssal Deep**: [UNKNOWN] — charts mark this region of open ocean with warnings. Ships that sail too far from known routes do not return.

## §mountain-ranges

- **The Spine of the World**: A massive range running north-south through the central continent. Contains the highest known peaks. Many peaks hold Legacy installations, some still active.
- **The Ironteeth**: A jagged, mineral-rich range in the northwest. Home to extensive mining operations and dwarven holds.
- **The Glasspeaks**: Mountains fused to translucent crystal by an ancient weapon. Beautiful and deadly — the glass amplifies sunlight to lethal intensity at certain angles.
- **The Shuddering Range**: Geologically active mountains in the southeast. Volcanic eruptions are common. The volcanoes are [UNKNOWN] — possibly artificial, maintained by deep Legacy systems.
- **The Pale Heights**: Snow-capped mountains in the far north. Largely unexplored. Expeditions report ruins of non-human origin.

## §great-rivers

- **The Varenthar**: The longest known river, flowing from the Spine of the World to the Sunward Sea. Supports dozens of cities along its banks.
- **The Blackflow**: A river whose waters run dark with dissolved Legacy minerals. Not poisonous, but gives the water an unsettling appearance. Excellent for agriculture.
- **The Twin Serpents**: Two parallel rivers that flow in opposite directions through the same valley, defying normal hydrology. Likely an artifact of Titan-era engineering.
- **The Greywater**: A wide, slow river crossing the central plains. Major trade artery.
- **The Torrent**: A fast, dangerous river descending from the Ironteeth. Rich in placer gold and Legacy metal fragments.

## §climate-zones

- **Northern Reaches**: Subarctic to arctic. Long winters, brief summers. Sparse population, hardy cultures.
- **Temperate Heartlands**: The most populated zone. Four distinct seasons. Mixed forest and farmland.
- **Southern Warmlands**: Mediterranean to subtropical. Dry summers, mild winters. Ancient olive groves and vineyards.
- **The Interior Wastes**: Arid desert and scrubland. Created by ancient devastation, slowly recovering. Nomadic cultures predominate.
- **Tropical Coasts**: Hot, humid, densely forested. Rich biodiversity, including many Legacy species. Difficult terrain for large-scale settlement.
- **The Blighted Zones**: Areas where sorcerous contamination has permanently altered the climate. Effects vary: perpetual fog, unnatural heat, reversed seasons. Avoid unless desperate. [UNKNOWN] number of these zones exist.

## §notable-landmarks

- **The Titan's Throne**: A mountain carved into the shape of a seated figure, visible from fifty miles. Origin and purpose: [UNKNOWN].
- **The Glass Desert**: A vast expanse of fused sand, created by an ancient weapon. Nothing grows there. At night, lights move beneath the surface.
- **The Worldwound**: A canyon of impossible depth. No expedition has reached the bottom. Sounds echo up from the darkness — machinery, voices, or [UNKNOWN].
- **The Eternal Storm**: A perpetual thunderstorm over the central ocean. Likely maintained by a Legacy weather-control system. Ships give it wide berth.
- **The Standing Stones of Varath**: Thousands of carved monoliths arranged in geometric patterns across a plain. Their purpose is [UNKNOWN], but they hum at certain times of year.
- **The Drowned Forest**: An underwater forest in the Jade Gulf, preserved by Legacy enchantment. The trees still bear leaves and fruit, though submerged for millennia.
