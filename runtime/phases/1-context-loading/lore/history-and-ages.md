# History and Ages of the Latter Earth

## §overview

The history of the Latter Earth stretches beyond mortal reckoning. Scholars divide the known past into Ages, though the boundaries between them are blurred by catastrophe and lost records. What follows is the consensus of the most learned sages — incomplete, disputed, and possibly wrong in fundamental ways.

## §the-first-age

**The Age of Shaping** — [UNKNOWN — almost entirely lost to time]

The earliest age defies reconstruction. The deepest Legacy ruins contain artifacts made from materials that do not exist in nature and cannot be replicated. Some scholars believe the First Age was when reality itself was constructed or significantly altered. Others hold that the world existed in a formless state before the Shapers — whoever or whatever they were — imposed order upon it.

What is known:
- The fundamental laws of nature may have been established or modified during this age
- The deepest ruins predate all other construction by an incomprehensible margin
- [UNKNOWN] entities of vast power were active during this period
- The transition to the Second Age involved a catastrophe so total that it left no coherent records

## §the-second-age

**The Age of Titans**

The Second Age saw the rise of beings of immense power — whether evolved, created, or ascended from mortal stock is [UNKNOWN]. These Titans reshaped continents, engineered new species, and constructed works of staggering ambition. Many of the Latter Earth's mountain ranges, inland seas, and great chasms date to this period.

The Titans waged wars that scarred the world permanently. Entire biomes were created as weapons or defenses. The great deserts of the interior were once fertile lands before Titan conflict rendered them barren. Legacy creatures from this age — those that survive — are among the most dangerous beings in existence.

The Second Age ended when the Titans destroyed themselves. The mechanism of their destruction is [UNKNOWN], but the aftermath reshaped the world's geography and seeded the residual energies that make magic possible.

## §the-third-age

**The Age of Sorcerer-Kings**

From the ashes of the Titan wars rose humanity and its kindred species. The Third Age saw mortal civilizations discover and harness the residual energies left by the Titans. The most powerful practitioners became the Sorcerer-Kings: rulers whose magical might made them effectively immortal and nearly omnipotent within their domains.

The Sorcerer-Kings built vast empires. They created servitor races, engineered plagues to destroy rivals, constructed pocket dimensions as personal retreats, and waged wars that lasted centuries. Their architectural and magical works form the majority of explorable Legacy ruins in the current age.

Key developments:
- Codification of the major magical traditions
- Creation of several non-human species still extant today
- Establishment of trade networks spanning continents
- Construction of the Great Roads, many of which still function
- The slow corruption of the Sorcerer-Kings by their own power

The Third Age ended in the Wars of Unmaking, when rival Sorcerer-Kings deployed weapons capable of unraveling the fabric of reality. Entire regions were erased from existence. The survivors retreated into hidden sanctums, and civilization collapsed.

## §the-fourth-age

**The Age of Embers**

A long, dark period of recovery. Scattered human communities rebuilt without the guidance (or tyranny) of the Sorcerer-Kings. New kingdoms formed, fell, and reformed. Knowledge was lost and partially recovered in cycles.

The Fourth Age lasted approximately two millennia. It saw:
- The founding of the first post-Sorcerer-King cities
- Rediscovery of basic magical techniques by new practitioners
- Wars fought with conventional weapons over Legacy resources
- The rise of organized religions, many centered on dimly-remembered Sorcerer-Kings or Titans
- Gradual stabilization of trade and agriculture

The Fourth Age ended with the Shattering — a catastrophe whose nature is [UNKNOWN]. Some scholars believe a Sorcerer-King awoke from hibernation and attempted to reclaim dominion. Others blame a Legacy weapon that activated autonomously. Still others point to signs of extraplanar invasion. The truth may encompass all of these or none.

## §the-fifth-age

**The Current Age — The Age of Rebuilding**

The current age began roughly six centuries ago, in the aftermath of the Shattering. It is defined by:

- Fragmented political landscape: no single empire dominates
- Medieval-level technology with Legacy artifacts as exceptions
- Growing scholarship and tentative exploration of ruins
- Increasing contact between distant cultures via renewed trade
- Rising tensions as expanding nations encounter one another
- Rumors of Sorcerer-Kings stirring in their hidden vaults: [UNKNOWN if true]

The current age is young. Its outcome is unwritten.
